# Formal relationship to Pattern Map v16

## Relationship statement

Pattern Map v16 is the broad Pattern Recognition / Discrimination Layer work.
The Echo Problem / ECHO-01 is a separate origin-accounting project derived from
the exact v15.2 checkpoint. In v16 it may appear as one worked example of why
repeated reports are not automatically independent corroboration, and as a
research track that can constrain claims about common-origin recurrence. It is
not v16's opening, definition, public map, mandatory architecture, or evidence
of effectiveness.

The relation is directional and bounded:

```text
v16 broad six-family framework
        │ may link to one bounded common-origin example
        ▼
The Echo Problem / EP v0.1
        │ may constrain origin-accounting claims and research questions
        └── cannot redefine v16, replace its six families, or supply results
```

## Shared concepts, separate authority

The projects can share language about source roles, recurrence, structured
comparison, uncertainty, memory, and human correction. They do not share
evidentiary status. A preserved v15.2 protocol remains a protocol; an
accessioned fixture or harness remains a fixture or harness; a site remains a
review surface; and a QA pass remains an integrity check. None becomes proof
that the broad framework works.

Common origin is also not the same as falsity, and independent origin is not
the same as truth. Provenance can constrain an interpretation without
establishing the claim. Technical access is not permission to use a source, and
a human disposition such as `HOLD`, `VERIFY`, or `QUARANTINE` is not an
observation about the world.

## Firebreak tests

- **Remove Echo:** v16 must still present the human problem, thesis, all six
  families, implementation spectrum, agent procedures, and human-judgment
  boundary without this project.
- **Remove v16:** EP v0.1 must still identify its origin-accounting question,
  source checkpoint, protocol, harness, fixtures, prior art, no-results state,
  and future execution gates without redefining the broad framework.
- **Keep v15.2 historical:** v15.2 names, source paths, hashes, and no-results
  state remain unchanged in the accession. New terminology belongs here or in
  adjacent accession metadata.
- **Keep unknown unknown:** an unresolved origin relation is represented as
  unknown or held, not filled by recurrence, proximity, or intuition.

## Out of scope for EP v0.1

EP v0.1 does not edit canonical v16 prose, framework, site, or decision logs;
does not claim the six families are newly invented; does not select a model or
provider; and does not grant authorization for an empirical, model,
participant, deployment, publication, or external-action workflow.

## EP v1.1 research-order clarification

The active EP v1.1 design checkpoint does not make origin accounting the v16
thesis. The controlled F0/F1/F2 cue-use design remains the first-paper
candidate for ECHO-01. A typed, graded, real-world dependence measurement
instrument is a prospective second-paper candidate only after the targeted
prior-measurement route, labelled instrument validation, rights/version review,
and a new owner authorization. Neither ordering is a completed paper or a
result.
