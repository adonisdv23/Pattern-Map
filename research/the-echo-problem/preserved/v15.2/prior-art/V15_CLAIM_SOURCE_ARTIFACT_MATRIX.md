## Reconciliation procedure

1. Add or amend rows when a lane identifies a material claim or closer source.
2. Cite exact primary URLs in the relevant research memo; do not use this matrix
   as a substitute bibliography.
3. Mark each change `ACCEPT`, `MODIFY`, `DEFER`, or `REJECT` in the decision
   ledger before propagating it.
4. Reconcile the same wording boundary across manuscript, site, PDF, protocol,
   fixtures, tests, owner packet, and transfer guide.
5. Add an empirical-result row only after an authorized study exists. At
   present, none does.
