03Mechanisms

Complete path

Eleven responsibilities, each open to inspection.

The map is deliberately more explicit than an eventual interface. Expand any component for its inputs, outputs, dependencies, failure modes, evidence boundary, and unresolved questions.

Family 01

Intent + authorization

What are we deciding, what is allowed, and what can it cost?

Principal outputA versioned decision brief and permission envelope.

C01Decision brief and authorization envelopeA versioned brief for the decision, permissions, expected baselines, and budget.Evidence status · Prior art + synthesisInspect

C01 · eight-field inspection record

Why it exists
Relevance and meaningful absence are task-relative. Acquisition, disclosure, stopping, and escalation need an explicit frame.
Consumes
The owner’s question; audience and use; constraints; permissions; time, money, token, compute, and attention limits; expected sources or perspectives.
Produces
A brief version, permission policy, expected baseline, cost envelope, and success, abstention, or escalation criteria.
How it interacts
Every later record points to this version. It constrains acquisition, defines relevance, gives gaps a baseline, and predefines outcomes.
What can go wrong
Technical access becomes mistaken for authorization; a vague question makes relevance arbitrary; a baseline is invented after the evidence; a changed brief fails to invalidate old work.
Example
IllustrativeAuthorize public-source research and a synthetic-data sandbox; prohibit production data; limit the question to whether a pilot is warranted.
Evidence boundary
Prior art + synthesisDecision analysis, value-of-information, mixed-initiative design, and risk-management practice support explicit task, cost, and authority framing—not this exact schema.
What remains speculative
UnresolvedThe minimum useful brief and whether one representation can travel across domains without flattening different evidence and permission rules.

Family 02

Evidence spine

What did we acquire, from whom, in which version?

Principal outputGoverned capture plus stable identity, normalization, and provenance records.

C02Acquisition controllerA governed controller for proposing, authorizing, recording, and stopping acquisition.Evidence status · Prior art + design hypothesisInspect

C02 · eight-field inspection record

Why it exists
One more search consumes resources and may cross privacy or permission boundaries. Search therefore needs both value and stopping logic.
Consumes
The brief; candidate sources, queries, and tools; current gaps and uncertainty; expected improvement; remaining budget.
Produces
An acquisition proposal, authorization result, capture or failure receipt, immutable raw-artifact reference, and budget update.
How it interacts
It receives targeted gaps from the graphs and router, gives captures to the evidence spine, and reports costs and failures.
What can go wrong
Novelty becomes value; scope expands silently; paid or sensitive retrieval runs without permission; failed capture becomes negative evidence; search has no stop state.
Example
IllustrativeAuthorize one targeted search for a separately authored rollback test—whose origin relation still must be assessed—instead of another broad search for product praise.
Evidence boundary
Prior art + design hypothesisInformation foraging, relevance feedback, active learning, value of information, and metareasoning are direct precedents for bounded selection.
What remains speculative
UnresolvedReliable value estimates in open-world research, especially with asymmetric harm and rare decisive evidence.

C03Source, artifact, normalization, and provenance spineStable source and artifact identities with append-only provenance.Evidence status · Prior art + synthesisInspect

C03 · eight-field inspection record

Why it exists
A source is not an artifact; a mutable page is not timeless; a normalized extract is not the original; a summary should not gain authority by losing its origin.
Consumes
Raw captures, source observations, artifact bytes or stable references, transformation specifications, and tool versions.
Produces
Source and artifact identities, versions or hashes, normalized representations, provenance edges, and explicit identity ambiguity.
How it interacts
It grounds both graphs, travels with context packets and memory, and lets a correction point to exact evidence.
What can go wrong
False merges, duplicate counting, qualifier loss, provenance laundering, unversioned page changes, and lineage presented as truth.
Example
IllustrativeKeep the vendor, documentation page, capture time, page version, extracted text, and parser version linked but distinct.
Evidence boundary
Prior art + synthesisProvenance standards such as W3C PROV-O, data lineage, and evidence-synthesis practice support traceable derivation.
What remains speculative
UnresolvedReliable identity resolution across aliases and complete provenance through opaque provider transformations.

Family 03

Relationships + claims

What is repeated, dependent, supported, contradicted, or missing?

Principal outputTyped origin, recurrence, gap, claim, evidence, and contradiction relationships.

C04Relationship, recurrence, common-origin, and gap graphTyped relationships for recurrence, common origin, time, and meaningful gaps.Evidence status · Prior art + design hypothesisInspect

C04 · eight-field inspection record

Why it exists
Repetition does not establish distinct-origin support. Velocity needs repeated observations; meaningful absence needs an expected baseline.
Consumes
Identified sources and artifacts, provenance, timestamps, grouping rules, baselines, similarity observations, and citations.
Produces
Typed relationships, candidate common-origin clusters, dependence-aware recurrence, comparison sets, gaps, temporal observations, and signal candidates.
How it interacts
It informs claim-level origin relations, shapes attention and enrichment, and can request targeted acquisition.
What can go wrong
Copies become votes; unknown origin becomes separately rooted; similarity becomes shared cause; one timestamp becomes velocity; an unspecified expectation manufactures absence.
Example
IllustrativeNine launch articles remain nine observations, but all link to one announcement rather than counting as nine confirmations.
Evidence boundary
Prior art + design hypothesisEvidence synthesis, provenance, sensemaking, and coordinated-amplification research support the boundary.
What remains speculative
UnresolvedUseful partial-dependence estimates and defensible thresholds for recurrence, velocity, gaps, or coordination.

C05Claim, evidence, comparison, and contradiction graphClaim-level support, contradiction, qualification, and comparison.Evidence status · Prior art + synthesisInspect

C05 · eight-field inspection record

Why it exists
One document can contain differently supported claims. Publisher reputation and citation presence cannot substitute for entailment.
Consumes
Exact artifact spans, candidate claims, identity and common-origin relationships, domain evidence standards, and comparison frames.
Produces
Claim versions; support, contradiction, qualification, and insufficiency edges; rationales; alternatives; and comparison matrices.
How it interacts
It receives provenance and origin-dependence context, supplies claim support without deciding action priority, and feeds cited material to the packet.
What can go wrong
Claims stay too broad; citation becomes entailment; authority transfers across claims; lexical overlap becomes evidence; open-world unknowns become binary verdicts.
Example
Illustrative‘Rollback is supported’ links to documentation; ‘rollback is reliable under interruption’ remains insufficient until that condition is tested.
Evidence boundary
Prior art + synthesisFEVER, SciFact, FActScore, evidence synthesis, and structured analytic techniques establish strong claim-level precedents.
What remains speculative
UnresolvedDomain-general claim decomposition and evidence standards that transfer without erasing expert judgment.

Family 04

Discrimination policy

Which judgment applies, and what permitted step should happen next?

Principal outputSeparate assessments, a cost-bounded route, and an inspectable context packet.

C06Multidimensional assessmentSeparate task-scoped judgments that must not collapse into one score.Evidence status · Conceptual synthesisInspect

C06 · eight-field inspection record

Why it exists
Compressing unlike judgments into one score hides where an error began and lets one virtue launder another.
Consumes
The brief, source and artifact records, both graphs, uncertainty, and possible consequences.
Produces
Typed assessments with reasons and evidence, unknown or contested states, action considerations, and a review queue.
How it interacts
It converts evidence structure into routing inputs while keeping each dimension open to correction.
What can go wrong
A trust score becomes a verdict; owner interest becomes endorsement; confidence substitutes for support; precise numbers hide uncertain origin.
Example
IllustrativeOfficial documentation can be authoritative for a documented feature, vendor-linked under the stated origin rule, highly relevant, and insufficient for real-world reliability at once.
Evidence boundary
Conceptual synthesisSource-credibility, epistemic-vigilance, claim-verification, relevance, and calibration research support the separations.
What remains speculative
UnresolvedWhether people and models can apply the dimensions reliably and whether the added explicitness improves outcomes enough to justify its cost.

C07 · eight-field inspection record

Why it exists
Assessment matters only if it guides a bounded step, but an action decision must not masquerade as a factual conclusion.
Consumes
Separate assessments, gap states, allowed actions, remaining resources, expected benefit and consequence, deadline, and stopping criteria.
Produces
A recommended action, alternatives, reason, expected benefit and cost range, uncertainty, and a stop or escalation receipt.
How it interacts
It can loop to acquisition, ask a person for permission, route evidence to packaging, and log predictions and costs for later comparison.
What can go wrong
An enrichment-value estimate becomes acceptance or dictates action priority; utility creates false precision; search stops at convenience or never stops; the route exceeds authorization.
Example

Family 06

Outcome feedback

What happened later, and should any policy change?

Principal outputAn expected-versus-observed comparison and a proposed, reviewable update.

C11Outcome feedback and revisable policy updateOutcome comparison that can propose—but never silently apply—a policy update.Evidence status · Design + empirical hypothesisInspect

C11 · eight-field inspection record

Why it exists
Without a defined outcome, feedback becomes retrospective storytelling. Without versioning, learning rewrites history.
Consumes
The original brief, route prediction, disposition, output, predefined outcome and horizon, observed result, confounders, and review authority.
Produces
An outcome record, attribution limits, error or calibration signal, and an approve/reject/defer proposal for policy update.
How it interacts
It reads the preserved decision state, returns a proposed update to human review, and never alters raw evidence or prior receipts.
What can go wrong
A noisy proxy becomes truth; hindsight changes the target; one owner’s preference becomes a global prior; contamination, selective follow-up, or drift compounds.
Example
IllustrativeA sandbox rollback outcome may revise the rule for a later pilot decision; it does not establish that the tool is generally reliable or unreliable.
Evidence boundary
Design + empirical hypothesisCalibration, organizational learning, decision analysis, and versioned governance are precedents for measured, reviewable updates.
What remains speculative
UnresolvedCausal attribution, useful update rates, safe transfer across tasks, and whether feedback improves policy rather than merely reinforcing local taste.

04Connections

Two loops · one preserved history

The flow is not a conveyor belt.

A gap can trigger another acquisition. A reviewer can revise the question. A failed capture can end a branch. An outcome can propose—but never silently apply—a new policy.

Three implementation paths

Different placements, not maturity levels.

The responsibility can live in team practice, workflow coordination, model behavior, or a combination. None is inherently deeper or more defensible.

PracticeMake the judgment explicit in existing work.

Define the decision, separate evidential judgments, record material exclusions, and state why research stopped.

SystemCoordinate evidence, policy, review, and memory.

Assemble an inspectable context packet while preserving provenance, permissions, and correction.

ModelEncourage information seeking and abstention.

Training or agent policies may help, but cannot replace external identity, receipts, permissions, or human authority.

06Challenges & limits

Failure is part of the specification

A serious framework names how it could lose.

Challenge 01

Old work, new label

The mechanisms reviewed here already have mature precedents. The plausible contribution is a boundary-preserving synthesis and an evaluation agenda—not a new mechanism family.

Challenge 02

A new gatekeeper

Any selection policy can reinforce institutional bias or erase peripheral evidence. Exclusions, unknowns, reasons, appeal, and source diversity must stay inspectable.

Challenge 03

Rigor theater

Detailed provenance can trace a false claim perfectly. Lineage never upgrades correctness, support, or permission by itself.

Challenge 04

More cost than value

The architecture may be too slow and cognitively heavy. It must beat strong simple baselines under matched time, tokens, retrieval, and review effort.

Challenge 05

Decorative human review

A person placed after an opaque route may only rubber-stamp it. Review must expose the evidence path and permit a consequential correction.

Challenge 06

Learning the wrong lesson

Outcome feedback can encode preference, contaminated proxies, or hindsight. Updates need a predefined outcome, attribution limits, versioning, and approval.

What would materially weaken or retire the framework for a named task class?

  • A strong retrieval-plus-citation baseline performs equivalently at lower cost.
  • Reviewers cannot reliably distinguish authority, support, origin relation, relevance, and action priority.
  • Origin-aware grouping hides legitimate convergence as often as it prevents false corroboration.
  • The interface increases overload, review time, or overreliance enough to erase any benefit.
  • Outcome updates encode local preference or contaminated proxies rather than better policy.

Current limitations

Sixteen boundaries the presentation cannot smooth away.

  1. No empirical evaluation.This project reports no experiment, participant study, field outcome, or comparative performance result.
  2. No broad mechanism novelty.The components have extensive prior art. The residual contribution is a boundary-preserving synthesis and one narrow supplied-cue hypothesis.
  3. No proven minimum.Eleven components are an analytical decomposition, not evidence that every task needs eleven implemented modules.
  4. No validated constructs.Reviewers may not reliably distinguish authority, support, origin relation, relevance, attention, enrichment value, action priority, and disposition.
  5. No provenance discovery.The proposed study supplies benchmark relations; it does not infer a real source path.
  6. No real-world independence.INDP means separate origin only as stipulated in the synthetic benchmark graph.
  7. Open-world evidence remains hard.Sources change, origins are obscured, important evidence is inaccessible, and support can remain contested.
  8. Costs and utilities are uncertain.A clean stopping rule can still stop before rare decisive evidence or encode the wrong consequence model.
  9. Human control is not guaranteed.Interfaces and organizational incentives can turn review into ceremony.
  10. Memory can amplify error.Retention, retrieval, and summarization can preserve stale, biased, or manipulated content.
  11. Surface leakage is not cleared.The small local smoke corpus is trivially separable; a blocked classifier and semantic audit remain stop gates.
  12. Transfer is unresolved.Public syndication resources do not supply the required claim/origin ground truth, and rights gates remain.
  13. The name may fail.Discrimination layer may remain ambiguous or harmful despite an explicit technical definition.
  14. The historical HTML is still pending.The original v13 diagram is now preserved and its supplied hash is verified; the expected standalone HTML remains unavailable and unverified.
  15. Product cases are illustrative.Alpha Solver and Signal Foundry are related cases, not independent validation.
  16. No publication or owner approval.This is a local review draft.

Terminology test: if representative readers keep inferring a social-classification thesis after the definition, rename it. Context judgment layer is the current clean alternative.
