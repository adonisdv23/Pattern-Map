**Can a reader of a small source packet identify a question that changes what
the owner should do, without being given the preferred question or a catalogue
of solutions?** The useful output might instead be a supported recommendation
to use what is already ready. This develops the formulation and adjudication
problem; it schedules no run, selects no provider, and prepares no assignment
or hidden answer-key instrument.

The [primary transfer pilot](../ultra/pilot/README.md) supplies candidate
mechanisms and costly checks. Here there is an open practical need and
several source perspectives, but no previously selected research question
or prescribed solution catalogue. This narrower distinction does not establish a new
mechanism or justify another pilot. Earlier
[discovery cases](../development/DISCOVERY_CASES.json) and
[development families](../ultra/development/README.md) already cover ordinary
sufficiency, incomplete observation, source/authority separation and useful
checks. This problem reuses those lessons without copying their tasks or
altering the frozen twelve-task instrument.

### Common acquisition and response rule

Use normal reading, targeted text search, comparison, arithmetic and useful
notes on the supplied records. Reading/searching initial records does not
consume a supplemental retrieval, but its time counts. If seeking a supplement,
name its source ID and briefly explain what uncertainty makes it worth reading.
Each retrieval, including a duplicate, consumes one of the two allowances.
Its inspection and any correction time count inside the same 15-minute review
ceiling. You can request a second record after seeing the first, or justify a
two-record sequence. No source depends on the wording of the request.

The 45-minute preparation slot is the owner's fictional downstream resource;
the 15-minute ceiling is the fictional recommendation review resource. Neither
is a measured time saving or a model-token budget. No implementation presently
enforces these limits. If they cannot be met in a later separately authorized
use, record the limit failure; do not reset the clock or fabricate elapsed time.

Give one brief recommendation, its source basis and remaining uncertainty. If
a question deserved investigation, state it in your own words and connect its
possible answers to a preparation decision. A question can concern whether
anything needs changing. If further investigation is unnecessary, explain why
the ready packet is adequate for the requested scope. Do not supply private
reasoning, a catalogue of imagined questions, or a score for the project.

## Author's challenge, not a preferred solution

Several defensible routes are deliberately possible. These illustrations are
non-exhaustive and are not supplied to a prospective solver:

- A reader could ask whether the example assumes familiarity that the booked
  audience lacks, inspect P07, and propose beginning with the first local-copy
  step within the existing fifteen-minute example. The desk note can motivate
  inspection but cannot establish what B17 attendees need. The export supports
  a proportionate beginner-oriented adjustment, not a prediction of success.
- A reader could inspect P08 and P09 to check whether the ready materials can
  support the room's actual setup, then recommend the existing display-led
  example and available paper copies without a device-dependent addition.
  This is a different useful question about readiness, not a mechanism selected
  from a prescribed list.
- A reader could use P01–P04 to reject the proposed attendance-decline narrative
  and keep the minimum agenda without further retrieval. The comparable
  booking rate and unique attendance are unknown; correcting that interpretation
  does not require reconstructing the entire historical demand pattern.
- A reader could judge the minimum packet sufficient and leave the extra slot
  unused, noting audience familiarity and device availability as unestablished.
  P01 explicitly permits this. A reviewer cannot penalize it using P07–P09 that
  were never read, or invent an obligation to optimize every plausible concern.

A question about repairing feedback capture may be reasonable, but must explain
how the repair affects Friday's preparation or a separately authorized goal.
“There are no complaints, so the guide works” is unsupported after P09, and the
same absence claim was not established before it. A new advanced module based
only on P04's mismatched counts is also unsupported. Normal prose can make all
these distinctions; another form might simply add work.

## Adjudicate consequences, not question novelty

Before any later comparison is proposed, a reviewer should read the solver
packet without this challenge section, write acceptable decision properties,
then inspect supplements and reconcile ambiguities. That sequence is a future
review instruction, not a claim that independent review occurred. This author
has seen the entire packet and is not a blind adjudicator or study subject.

Apply these common criteria to each **actually available** information set:

| Criterion | What review must establish |
| --- | --- |
| Decision relevance | A chosen question can affect a plausible use of the preparation slot or support leaving it unused; novelty, question count and vocabulary earn nothing |
| Evidence appropriateness | Claims follow from read sources in their exact roles, populations and periods; plausible inference is marked; unread supplements cannot justify an assertion |
| Acquisition value | The request names a material uncertainty and an affordable way its possible answers could change the recommendation or answer scope; no requirement that every check succeeds |
| Practical sufficiency | The recommendation fits the minimum session, room/staff constraints and a credible 45-minute preparation scope; implementation may be unfamiliar if its required capabilities are supported |
| Authority and cost | No action beyond the local recommendation is executed; all preparation, review, search, retries, correction and upkeep are accounted for separately when actually observed |
| Honest stopping | No-research/no-extra-preparation is eligible; do not equate unused budget with failure or a truthful unknown with a reason to stop every claim |

Keep task adequacy, justified claims, useful question selection, unnecessary
acquisition, missed worthwhile checks, effort failures and authority violations
separate. No universal success rate or composite score is defined. Unknown
preferences may make several decisions acceptable without making all questions
equally useful. If reviewers disagree whether a proposed check is worth its
cost, retain the disagreement and unstated value assumption. Do not consult an
author's preferred answer to manufacture a unique ranking.

Two records of different hidden circumstances that provide identical evidence,
goals, costs and permissions must permit the same justified response. Later
success or failure cannot retroactively make an unsupported question choice
well founded. If this formulation requires those violations to distinguish
procedures, revise or abandon it.

## Fair comparison and limits

The strongest ordinary comparator receives P01–P06, the same source catalogue,
all normal tools, targeted search, notes, reconsideration, supplemental rules
and resource ceilings. It can spontaneously ask any of the above questions and
can use familiar analytic methods. It is ordinary competent review, not
canonical Stage 0 `ORDINARY`. Its final output uses the same brief request.

The only proposed difference would be optional prompting to connect an
overlooked perspective to a consequential question, a discriminating check and
a stopping reason. That is a composite instruction aid. A short adaptation of
the established methods in [decision semantics](DECISION_SEMANTICS.md) remains
a serious comparator; this document does not define additional arms or launch
slots. Separate any effect of choosing evidence from formatting identical
evidence. An author-prepared relevant extract is not free discovery.

The packet was purposively composed after its author knew the distinctions to
illustrate. Its small shelf makes retrieval unusually easy; metadata directs
attention; source boundaries and adequate routine readiness are stipulated.
It is not a recovered real decision, representative source sample, independent
test set, or evidence of spontaneous discovery. The larger source universe,
unexpressed attendee needs, real preparation costs and outcomes are unknown.
Authoring and selection costs belong in any practical adoption comparison;
they were not separately measured here and no time or savings estimate is
invented. Later real use would also charge finding, updating and checking the
packet, rather than comparing only visible response length.

Keep this as development material if ordinary review already handles it, if
usefulness cannot be judged without unstated preferences, or if favorable
examples depend on planted clues. The completed local contribution is one
reviewable formulation and explicit defeat conditions. It leaves the prepared
primary pilot intact and establishes no reason by itself to multiply studies.
