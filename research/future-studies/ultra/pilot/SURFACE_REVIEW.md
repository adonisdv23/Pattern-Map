### Material grading finding

**S-K01 — Accepted with revision; exact private clarification verified below.**
The initial global component-separation clause said that an accurate unknown
after wasted inspections “can fail task acceptance,” without defining the
primary binary outcome for a plausible unsuccessful acquisition. For example,
T001 may inspect the publicly plausible eager-lookup record first, learn that
it misses the limit, and correctly HOLD because the remaining one unit cannot
establish both manifest obligations. The statement's factual support is clear;
the initial rule left task acceptance discretionary.

The task author accepted a proposed distinction: primary attainment of a route
that was reachable at episode start versus epistemic appropriateness of the
terminal statement. In initially attainable tasks, such a HOLD receives zero
attainment while retaining correct-unknown credit. The unsuccessful inspection
is not automatically an ex ante avoidable-error harm. In initially unattainable
gap tasks, a supported bounded HOLD/STOP can earn attainment credit. This value
framing remains a proposal for owner review, not empirical validation or an
assertion that a reasonable search decision deserved blame.
