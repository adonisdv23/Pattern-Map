## What to review

- The companion now asks which choice a proposed question could improve, keeps
  past reasons tied to their conditions, and preserves independently supported
  conclusions when a different claim remains uncertain. Existing permission,
  identity, consequence and memory requirements still apply. No new schema or
  mandatory record has been introduced.
- ORBIT illustrates a small useful source pointer and a later reason to discard
  redundant work. The sources, events and ordinary alternative are authored.
- [Decision semantics](../research/future-studies/decision-continuation/DECISION_SEMANTICS.md)
  explains necessary/sufficient reasons, preserved observations and scoped
  reconsideration. [Birch](../research/future-studies/decision-continuation/QUESTION_DISCOVERY_DEVELOPMENT.md)
  develops choosing a worthwhile question from an open need; doing no extra
  work remains valid. Neither is a new evaluated instrument.
- The execution packet proposes effect and separate cost choices for the
  unchanged primary pilot. Arithmetic is planning only. It does not select a
  provider, accept the owner's values, implement a provider transport or run
  any of the 144 assignments.
- Examples and Research expose the selected material through optional
  disclosures. Source-local links land on the appropriate document headings
  in review/public routed pages and standalone exports.

## What still requires real evidence or an owner decision

Actual mentor review, real operator use, observed effort, retrieval success and
causal benefit remain unknown. This candidate cannot supply those observations.
Physical keyboard, supported screen reader, real zoom, forced colors, hardware
touch and native print checks retain the earlier explicitly recorded residuals.
Automated checks and advisory review do not substitute for them.

The owner must settle task/rubric acceptance, exact provider/model/configuration,
value and resource choices, authorized reviewers, data handling, retention,
stop owner and expiry before a study. Runtime isolation and resource enforcement
must then be verified under that exact scope. Do not give the complete owner
package to a tested model; it contains authoring and rubric material.

Feature-branch and draft-PR review do not authorize main merge, deployment,
publication, outreach or study execution. Null, harmful, fragile, non-transfer,
stop and inconclusive results remain eligible in any eventual comparison.
