# One real Signal Foundry decision: prospective use script

Status: **PREPARED / NOT EXECUTED / NO OPERATOR OR PRODUCT OUTCOME RECORDED**.
Purpose: learn whether a small Pattern Map intervention earns its effort in one
actual decision using the existing Signal Foundry application. This is not a
controlled effectiveness study or permission to access a packet or press Apply.

Source inspected: Signal Foundry `f8066eef2029168734bb5dcd4dfb08ffcff216ce`,
`docs/decision_memory_retrospective_v1.md`, `app.py` decision-episode compose,
preview, Apply and subject-history routes, and the offline fixture assessment.
The earlier transfer's recipient and effect remain unverified.

## Before the use

Select one pending owner decision and one actual, available immutable evidence
packet through Daily Intelligence. Record the real canonical collection/record
and displayed packet digest; a plausible-looking reference is insufficient.
The owner chooses the environment, permitted read/write scope, and a tolerable
time allowance before the session. Start separate preparation and review timers.
If the packet is unavailable, record that limit and stop; do not infer absence.

Before applying extra structure, preserve the owner's plausible initial choice
and main reason. Select one intervention that could change it: inspect a missed
perspective, compare an alternative mechanism, challenge the key support, or
resolve a meaningful observation gap. Use existing available material within
the permitted scope. Record what was considered, what actually changed the
choice or its justification, and what would reverse it. Stop at the agreed
allowance, when the decision is adequately supported, or when further access
would exceed scope.

## Use the existing application

Open the item's **Record an operator decision** page. Its current compose form
requires a question, one to four hypotheses, an expected observable outcome,
the operator decision and rationale; unresolved and untried states are optional.
The two-event offline fixture does not prove that this complete UI flow fits
the task. Have the human author or approve each statement. Do not invent an
expectation merely to fill a required field: record disproportionate form
burden as a limitation if an honest entry does not fit.

Preview the exact words, canonical subject and evidence digest. Preview writes
no decision history. Only within the later authorized real-use scope may the
owner confirm the exact Preview with the application's literal `APPLY` step.
Retain the resulting episode and redacted receipt references. Resolve a stale
head through the existing re-preview route; never bypass the gate or copy
synthetic fixture statements into actual history.

After an intervening task, return through the canonical record's **Decision
Memory history**. Check whether the owner can recover why the earlier decision
was made, its unresolved condition and its source boundary. Record retrieval
effort and any wrong-subject, missing-reason or stale-context failure. Corrections
remain additive. A later actual outcome needs its genuine observation, later
immutable evidence and the existing expected-outcome binding; no outcome is
inferred from elapsed time or missing history.

## Record value and burden together

Capture one compact use note: decision and source references; initial/final
choice; actual intervention; useful alternative or corrected mistake if any;
unchanged/no-benefit finding; preparation, review and retrieval minutes; any
correction; and later outcome only when observed. The note should point to
existing history, not duplicate private statements in another canonical store.

One unchanged decision may still be well supported, but it does not establish
incremental benefit. One successful retrieval does not justify a new adapter.
Reopen typed reason queries or UI simplification only after repeated named
failures show the existing path cannot answer a real need at acceptable effort.
Keep a no-build or remove-the-extra-step outcome available.
