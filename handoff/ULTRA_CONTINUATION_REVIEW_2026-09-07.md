## What is different now

The leading research question is whether explicit transfer obligations and
selective evidence acquisition improve supported task outcomes beyond both
careful ordinary practice and explicit purpose/mechanism comparison under the
same resource ceilings. A simpler comparator winning would be useful. The
proposal makes no new-mechanism or effectiveness claim. Observation/capture
remains a narrower measurement question; its earlier first-pilot recommendation
and candidate packets are preserved as history, with the changed priority made
explicit in current navigation.

The [development collection](../research/future-studies/ultra/development/README.md)
contains eight distinct families and sixteen paired variants. It separates
assertions from actions, gives costs and constraints, tests when filtering order
commutes, and provides counterexamples involving incomplete capture, changing
transfer conditions, complementary components and unnecessary work. These are
authored specifications, not a benchmark result or independent observations.

The [pilot instrument](../research/future-studies/ultra/pilot/README.md) adds twelve
fresh fictional tasks across three domains and four regimes, private evidence
releases and semantic rubrics, two separately authored narratives over shared
structured facts, exact instructions and an offline action adapter. Its
144 planned slots are unlaunched. The adapter exposes only public views, records
all attempted actions and costs, and enforces finite acquisition/attempt limits.
It neither connects to a model nor grades semantic correctness. A future tested
model must not receive this owner package, which includes answer keys.

Advisory review changed the work materially: it clarified intermediate versus
terminal effort failure, removed an unspecified population interval, required
mechanism bindings for renamed implementations, corrected public action
availability and nested-field disclosure, counterbalanced candidate/inspection
IDs, and removed answer-bearing catalogue clues. The public-first narrative
review retained its original ambiguities before inspecting private keys.
The source notes preserve authoring judgments and the limits of those checks.
