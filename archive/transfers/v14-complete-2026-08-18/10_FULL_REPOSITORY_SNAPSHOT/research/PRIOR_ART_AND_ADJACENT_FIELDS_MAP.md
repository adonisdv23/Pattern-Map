# Prior art and adjacent-fields map

Status: expanded targeted map sufficient to bound the provisional thesis; the original v13 diagram is hash-verified, while a protocol-led review and the standalone v13 HTML remain future completeness gates.

Access date: 2026-08-18

## Bottom line

The provisional discrimination-layer thesis is not a clean-sheet invention. The mechanisms reviewed in this targeted map have mature precedents in adjacent fields: search and relevance feedback, information foraging, source credibility, claim verification, provenance, evidence synthesis, decision analysis, sensemaking, mixed-initiative systems, retrieval-augmented generation, agent memory, calibration, and organizational learning.

The plausible program-level contribution is narrower: a reader-friendly, testable policy contract that keeps those mechanisms connected while refusing to collapse their outputs into one score. Even “integrated policy” is not presumed novel. The immediate paper candidate is smaller still: whether a supplied typed origin relation improves origin accounting beyond an explicit rule on stipulated synthetic graphs. That remains an untested representation/use hypothesis.

## Field-by-field boundary

| Field | What it already explains | Relationship to this framework | Boundary that must remain visible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Information foraging | Search as adaptive movement among information patches under time and effort costs; information scent and gain-per-cost. [Pirolli & Card (1999)](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.106.4.643) | Direct precedent for deciding where to look next. | Search value is not truth, authority, or claim support. |
| Information retrieval and relevance feedback | Iterative retrieval can incorporate judgments about relevant and non-relevant items. [Rocchio (1971)](https://www.sigir.org/files/museum/pub-08/XXIII-1.pdf) | Direct precedent for query refinement and owner feedback. | Relevance is task-relative; it is not credibility or factual correctness. |
| Epistemic vigilance | People evaluate both communicated content and the source of communication. [Sperber et al. (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0017.2010.01394.x) | Supports separating source assessment from content assessment. | A source cue cannot replace claim-level examination. |
| Source credibility | Credibility assessment is multidimensional, contextual, and partly perceptual. [Metzger (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1002/asi.20672) | Supports domain-scoped authority and explicit uncertainty. | Perceived credibility is not universal trust or proof. |
| Claim verification | Claims can be decomposed and linked to evidence that supports, refutes, or leaves them unresolved. [FEVER](https://aclanthology.org/N18-1074/), [SciFact](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.609/), [FActScore](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/) | Direct precedent for atomic claims, rationales, and insufficient-evidence states. | Benchmark performance in bounded corpora does not establish open-world verification. |
| Intelligence analysis and structured analytic techniques | Key assumptions checks, competing hypotheses, indicators, red teams, and alternative futures make reasoning more explicit. [CIA Tradecraft Primer (2009)](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/books-monographs/a-tradecraft-primer/) | Supports comparison, contradiction, and inspectable human judgment. | A structured technique does not automatically acquire trustworthy evidence or remove analyst bias. |
| Sensemaking systems | Analysts move between foraging, collection, schema construction, evidence marshaling, and interpretation. [Pirolli & Card (2005)](https://andymatuschak.org/files/papers/Pirolli%2C%20Card%20-%202005%20-%20The%20sensemaking%20process%20and%20leverage%20points%20for%20analyst%20technology%20as.pdf), [Weick et al. (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.1050.0133) | Strong precedent for an iterative map rather than a linear pipeline. | Sensemaking can yield a plausible frame without yielding a true one. |
| Knowledge provenance | Entities, activities, agents, derivations, responsibility, and time can be represented and exchanged. [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) | Direct precedent for the evidence spine and derivation graph. | Lineage shows where something came from; it does not establish that the content is correct. |
| Scientific evidence and argument graphs | Claims, evidence, methods, interpretations, qualifications, support, and challenge relations can be represented explicitly. [SEE](https://doi.org/10.1186/2041-1480-5-S1-S1), [Micropublications](https://doi.org/10.1186/2041-1480-5-28) | Near-direct precedent for the claim/evidence portion of the proposed typed graph. | These biomedical/scientific models do not by themselves provide live acquisition policy, authorization, bounded routing, or human disposition. |
| Natural-language claim provenance | Claim-provenance graphs represent sources and approximate relations among evolving natural-language claims; inferred origin remains uncertain. [Zhang, Ives & Roth (2020)](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.406/) | The closest direct precedent for common-origin reasoning over reports and paraphrases. | Provenance inference can be incomplete or wrong; an unknown origin must remain unknown rather than becoming independent. |
| Argument-based decision support | Pendo represents questions, competing claims, pro/con evidence, weighted argument, reusable artifacts, and reported decision outcomes. [Introne & Iandoli (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dss.2014.04.005) | A close integrated precedent for connecting evidence representation with decisions and outcomes. | It does not supply the same provenance/dependence/authorization contract, but it prevents claiming that evidence-to-decision integration is new. |
| Provenance verification | ProVe selects passages from documented provenance, classifies support/refute/neutral relations, and aggregates source support while separating verifiability from truth. [Amaral, Rodrigues & Simperl (2024)](https://doi.org/10.3233/SW-233467) | A direct comparator for claim/span/provenance verification and evidence selection. | A provenance-supported assertion can still be false; the remaining project question is cross-source origin accounting or action policy. |
| Evidence synthesis | Search, eligibility, study identity, bias, synthesis, and updating are already disciplined workflows. [Cochrane Handbook](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current), [PRISMA 2020](https://www.bmj.com/content/372/bmj.n71.long) | Supports source grouping, explicit inclusion, updates, and auditability. | A general AI framework must not present evidence-synthesis primitives as new or transfer medical evidence rules indiscriminately. |
| Common-origin control | Multiple reports of one study must be collated rather than counted as independent studies. [Cochrane Handbook, chapter 4](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-04) | Concrete precedent for “recurrence is not independence.” | Shared origin can be unknown; unknown must not silently become independent. |
| Human-in-the-loop and mixed initiative | Systems should consider uncertainty, user goals, attention, costs, timing, correction, and control. [Horvitz (1999)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/principles-mixed-initiative-user-interfaces/), [Amershi et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300233) | Supports human override, clarification, abstention, and repair. | Merely inserting a reviewer does not prevent automation bias or rubber-stamping. |
| Evidence-path interfaces and reliance | Granular claim–evidence provenance can alter perceived trust while failing to change reliance or edit behavior, and dense displays can impose usability costs. [PaperTrail (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21045) | Directly motivates progressive disclosure and correction-focused evaluation rather than “more provenance” as an end. | One scholarly-QA study does not establish a universal interface effect; trust, behavior, correction, and workload must be measured separately. |
| Active learning | Systems can choose which examples to label under a limited annotation budget. [Settles (2009)](https://burrsettles.com/pub/settles.activelearning_20090109.pdf) | Precedent for uncertainty-driven acquisition. | Selecting training labels is not the same as selecting evidence for a live decision. |
| Value of information and metareasoning | Additional information or computation is justified when expected decision improvement exceeds its cost. [Howard (1966)](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSSC.1966.300074), [Russell & Wefald (1991)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(91)90015-C) | The strongest formal precedent for enrichment and stopping rules. | A usable implementation still needs utilities, uncertainties, and cost estimates; “more context” is not automatically valuable. |
| Agentic search control | Over-search and under-search are distinct measurable errors; uncertainty about whether to search can be related to search-decision quality. [Wu et al. (2025)](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.998/) | Supports an explicit reason to search, stop, hold, or clarify and a route receipt. | One method and benchmark suite do not validate a domain-general evidence policy or a particular uncertainty threshold. |
| Agentic research and graph memory | Current systems combine uncertainty-triggered or iterative search, document analysis, tool selection, and structured or short-term reasoning memory. [Agentic Reasoning (2025)](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.1383), [Search-o1 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.276), [DeepResearcher (2025)](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.22) | Makes “search + inspect + remember before generation” an untenable novelty claim. | These systems do not automatically preserve source-origin uncertainty, authorization, immutable evidence, or accountable human disposition. |
| Resource rationality | Accuracy must be traded against finite computational resources. [Lieder & Griffiths (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X1900061X) | Supports explicit latency, attention, money, and token budgets. | A bounded policy may still stop too early or encode the wrong utility. |
| Retrieval-augmented generation | Generation can condition on dynamically retrieved non-parametric information. [Lewis et al. (2020)](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/6b493230205f780e1bc26945df7481e5-Abstract.html) | Direct implementation context for pre-generation selection. | Retrieval similarity is not claim support, reliability, or authority. |
| Strong simple RAG baselines | A source-faithful retrieve-then-read baseline matched or outperformed more elaborate pipelines on the tested long-context QA benchmarks under scaled token budgets. [Laitenberger, Manning & Liu (2025)](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1656/) | Establishes the minimum comparison discipline for any claim that the added layer creates value. | This is not proof that simple RAG always wins; it requires matched-budget, task-specific comparison rather than a complexity prior. |
| Context engineering | Industry practice now treats instructions, tools, memory, retrieval, compression, and context lifecycle as a managed finite resource. [Anthropic (2025)](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents) | Near-direct overlap with the proposed pre-generation layer. | The label is recent; most underlying mechanisms are not. Industry guidance is not independent empirical validation. |
| Long-context behavior | Models may use information differently depending on its position and the amount of context. [Liu et al. (2024)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/) | Supports selection, ordering, compression, and bounded packets. | A longer context window does not remove selection or evaluation problems. |
| Agent memory | Agents can retain experiences, retrieve them, form reflections, link memories, and separate facts from opinions. [Park et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3586183.3606763), [A-Mem (2025)](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/19909c36f51abc4856b4560aff3d36d6-Abstract-Conference.html), [Hindsight (2026)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-demo.27/) | Strong overlap with preservation, update, and revisability. | Memory-system author claims and benchmark results require separate evaluation; stored material can propagate errors. |
| Source-aware RAG and context attribution | Recent systems explicitly estimate source reliability or attribute generated behavior to retrieved documents. [RA-RAG (2025)](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1738/), [Nematov et al. (2025 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04480) | Makes a generic “source-aware retrieval” novelty claim untenable. | Reliability inferred from agreement can fail under dependence or coordinated copying; attribution is not truth. |
| Integrated cross-source reasoning | HydraRAG combines graph topology, text, source reliability, and cross-source corroboration; CONFACT studies conflicting evidence and source credibility. [HydraRAG (2025)](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.730/), [CONFACT (2025)](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2025/1073) | Directly closes the broad novelty claim around source-aware corroboration or conflict-sensitive multi-source reasoning. | Neither isolates a supplied same/distinct/unresolved origin-family field against a byte-identical rule-only control. |
| Fact-level conflict and uncertainty relations | FaithfulRAG models fact-level conflict between retrieved and parametric knowledge; CLUE identifies claim–evidence and inter-evidence conflict/agreement relations for uncertainty explanations. [FaithfulRAG (2025)](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1062/), [CLUE (2026)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2110/) | Makes typed evidence relations and pre-generation conflict handling established neighboring mechanisms. | Conflict/agreement is a claim-stance or evidence-interaction relation, not the same as origin-family dependence. |
| Matched evidence-utilization and source-attribution diagnostics | Current work separates evidence availability from observable condition effects and attributes influence across redundant, complementary, or synergistic retrieved sources. [Xia (2026 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06758), [Nematov et al. (2025 preprint)](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.04480), [BERGEN (2024)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.449/), [KUP (2025)](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1326/) | Supplies the closest methods/source-influence precedents for an oracle relation-metadata condition test and a reason to expect null, negative, or direct-code effects. | Supplied evidence or metadata is not a guaranteed upper bound; source attribution is not an origin-family graph; the effect remains model-, task-, label-, and format-specific. |
| Authorization, poisoning, and metadata bias in RAG | Recent work treats data-use authorization, visible-source poisoning, and authority/authorship metadata as active RAG failure surfaces. [S-RAG (2025)](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.512), [RAG Paradox (2025)](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.findings-emnlp.1291), [Authority Bias (2025)](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.1400), [Attribution Bias (2025)](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.findings-acl.1087) | Requires the framework to distinguish declared policy, enforced authorization, and after-the-fact audit; visible provenance is dual-use. | Metadata and citations can distort or expose systems as well as help correction; transparency is not a monotonic safety benefit. |
| Memory security and provenance laundering | Recent preprints show how untrusted origin may be obscured through summarization, tool echo, or manufactured corroboration. [Louck (2026 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24322) | Supports origin binding across transformations and cautious authority elevation. | This is recent, unreviewed work; treat it as a threat model to test, not settled science. |
| Collective intelligence | Group performance depends on interaction structure, not simply the strongest individual. [Woolley et al. (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1193147) | Relevant to aggregating distributed judgment and designing human/model roles. | Agreement count and group performance are not independent corroboration; replication and construct questions remain. |
| Coordinated amplification and manipulation | Coordinated networks can manufacture apparent recurrence; information cascades can suppress private information. [Meta CIB definition](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/04/march-2021-coordinated-inauthentic-behavior-report/), [Banerjee (1992)](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/publications/banerjee92herd.pdf) | Supports common-origin and coordination checks before weighting recurrence. | Coordination signals are probabilistic; authentic coordination must not automatically be labeled deceptive. |
| Data lineage and governance | Standards describe lineage events, datasets, jobs, runs, governance, and risk-management responsibilities. [OpenLineage](https://openlineage.io/docs/), [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10) | Supports versioning, traceability, lifecycle controls, and enterprise translation. | Governance coverage does not establish content quality or compliance in a particular deployment. |
| Bayesian calibration | Confidence estimates should correspond to observed correctness frequencies. [Guo et al. (2017)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html) | Supports measuring rather than merely displaying confidence. | Calibrated model confidence is not source authority, evidence independence, or decision utility. |
| Organizational learning | Learning crosses individual, group, and organizational levels and may become embedded in routines. [Crossan et al. (1999)](https://doi.org/10.5465/amr.1999.2202135) | Supports outcome feedback, versioned priors, and correction over time. | Institutionalized memory can preserve a mistake; learning is not synonymous with truth. |
| Decision intelligence / decision quality | Decision practice separates framing, alternatives, information, values, reasoning, and action. [Society of Decision Professionals](https://www.decisionprofessionals.com/who-we-are/decision-quality) | Supports connecting evidence to consequence-sensitive action. | “Decision intelligence” is a broad practitioner label, not a single settled academic theory. |

## Strongest overlap that narrows the thesis

Four bodies of work materially constrain the maximum claim:

1. Value-of-information and metareasoning already formalize whether one more search or computation is worth its cost.
2. Sensemaking, evidence synthesis, provenance, and mixed-initiative research already connect collection, representation, comparison, human judgment, and revision.
3. Recent source-aware RAG and agent-memory systems already address reliability, attribution, fact/belief separation, temporal updating, and persistent context.
4. Pendo, ProVe, PaperTrail, and current agentic-search systems already integrate multiple pieces that the project initially treated as separate opportunities.

Accordingly, the framework should not claim a novel mechanism family. Its plausible contribution is a boundary-preserving synthesis and an evaluation agenda for the decision *before* generation.

## Plausible contribution boundary

The following are researchable possibilities, not established contributions:

- A typed end-to-end representation connecting source/artifact identity, claims, evidence spans, common origin, task relevance, action policy, and memory updates.
- A visible policy for `answer`, `answer provisionally`, `compare`, `acquire`, `clarify`, `hold`, `defer`, or `refuse` under monetary, latency, token, privacy, and human-attention budgets.
- An append-only evidence and decision ledger in which raw observations remain immutable while interpretations, dispositions, and priors can be revised.
- Origin-aware corroboration that discounts copied or coordinated material without treating all recurrence as manipulation.
- Evaluation centered on supported-claim rate, appropriate abstention, calibration, correction time, source diversity, provenance completeness, cost, and decision quality—not fluency alone.

## Unresolved novelty and evaluation questions

- What is the operative unit: source, artifact, passage, claim, event, observation, or memory?
- Can reviewers reliably distinguish authority, support, independence, relevance, attention, and action priority?
- Does keeping those dimensions separate improve decisions enough to justify the added complexity?
- How should source reliability update when sources copy one another, change ownership, or are selectively silent?
- How does provenance survive paraphrase, summarization, tool calls, embeddings, and memory consolidation?
- What measurable policy chooses between more acquisition, human escalation, provisional output, and withholding?
- Does the framework beat a simpler composition of ordinary retrieval, citations, and manual review under matched cost and time?
- Does common-origin analysis reduce false corroboration without hiding legitimate consensus?
- Does outcome feedback improve calibration, or merely encode one operator's preferences?

## Concepts that must not be claimed as original

Information foraging; relevance feedback; active learning; value of information; metareasoning; Bayesian calibration; epistemic vigilance; source credibility; structured analytic techniques; sensemaking; evidence synthesis; provenance and data lineage; RAG; source-aware retrieval; context engineering; context ordering/compression; agent memory and reflection; fact/belief separation; human-in-the-loop or mixed initiative; coordinated-amplification detection; organizational learning; and decision quality.

## Evidence posture

- Scholarly and standards sources above support only the claims attributed to their bounded fields.
- Industry guidance is used as implementation context, not independent validation.
- Preprints are labeled as preprints and used to expose overlap or threat models, not to establish settled findings.
- Model reviewers are not sources and are not cited as evidence.
