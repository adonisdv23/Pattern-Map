## What Signal Foundry may illustrate

The committed boundary documents are useful concrete examples of upstream evidence discipline:

- bounded intake and inventory;
- stable source and artifact identity;
- provenance and operation receipts;
- separation of content evidence, visual context, comments, related context, metadata, provider failures, and later derived analysis;
- grouping and recurrence without promoting recurrence to truth;
- gaps as typed review conditions rather than conclusions;
- enrichment selection under explicit authorization and cost boundaries;
- saved, unsaved, missing, and excluded states;
- human review and correction;
- a future pointer-based handoff to a downstream reasoning system.

Its “Pattern Candidate” concept is intentionally non-claiming: queue placement says an item may deserve inspection, not that it is true, valuable, ready, or actionable.

## Evidence classes that must remain separate

- Transcript evidence is not Visual Evidence.
- Visual Evidence is not transcript text.
- Comments and audience context are not proof of truth, demand, or value.
- Related context is not corroboration by default.
- Provider metadata and successful receipts are not content correctness.
- Velocity metadata is not proof of momentum or future performance.
- An absence/gap flag is not proof that the expected thing does not exist.
- A Pattern Candidate is not a conclusion, score, readiness state, or acquisition authorization.
- Downstream derived analysis is not original evidence.

These are useful framework distinctions because they show how a system can preserve heterogeneous context without flattening it into a single evidence class.

## What must not be universalized

Signal Foundry's transcript-first domain, object names, collections, queue fields, provider states, Cloud storage choices, and operator workflow are product-specific. They do not define the universal object model, required infrastructure, or required user interface for a discrimination layer.

The framework may borrow the *principle* of preserving evidence class, origin, exclusion, and review state. It may not infer that every implementation needs:

- videos or transcripts;
- Signal Foundry's routes, schemas, or storage backends;
- a Pattern Candidate Queue;
- the same receipt vocabulary;
- the same enrichment providers;
- an Alpha Solver handoff.
