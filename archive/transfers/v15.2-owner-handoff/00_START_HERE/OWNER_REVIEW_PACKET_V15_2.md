## The intended reader takeaway

After the short route, a thoughtful non-specialist should be able to say:

> An AI answer inherits choices made before it writes: which material was
> found, which reports were treated as separate support, what was allowed to
> influence the answer, and what was left unresolved. Those choices should be
> visible and correctable. Nine reports can still trace to one announcement;
> preserving all nine observations does not require pretending they are nine
> independent supports.

After the complete essay, three ideas should remain:

1. **Repetition is not automatically corroboration.** Nine reports can be nine
   observations while still representing one known information path.
2. **Different judgments must stay different.** Origin relation, claim support,
   authority, relevance, permission, action priority, and the owner's decision
   are related but cannot safely substitute for one another.
3. **The path into the answer should be inspectable.** A reader or operator
   should be able to see what changed the count or route, correct that judgment,
   and preserve the history.

The site should not leave the impression that every AI workflow needs this
machinery. It argues for a reviewable responsibility in evidence-sensitive
work, and for retiring it wherever a simpler method performs as well at lower
cost.

## What changed in v15.2

### Argument and voice

- The essay opens with the actual object and decision: nine reports, one
  announcement, and a proposed external action.
- The first receipt is reduced to `09 observations / 01 known shared path / 00
  separately rooted supports for the broad claim / HOLD`.
- Zero counted support is explained as a hold, not as proof that the underlying
  claim is false.
- The AI consequence is explicit: if the shared relationship disappears, the
  answer inherits inflated plurality.
- The core framework is organized around three questions instead of an
  exhaustive inventory in the main essay.
- The six families and eleven component records still exist in Explore for
  readers who need implementation depth.
- The prose preserves the project's strongest commitments: the correction
  invariant, the use boundary, the right to leave relations unresolved, the
  human next step, and the promise to report an unfavorable study honestly.

### Reading and comprehension

- The reader now offers honest cumulative stops at 60-90 seconds, about four
  minutes, and about nine minutes.
- High-friction terms can open a definition, a concrete example, and a boundary
  explaining what the term does not mean.
- Definitions are progressive disclosure, not repairs for otherwise opaque
  prose; the full glossary remains in Sources.
- `F0`, `F1`, `F2`, `T1`, `N=300`, `M=75`, `FC_cons`, `VOR`, provenance audit,
  runtime, human disposition, and relation-state codes are translated before
  or alongside their short labels.
- Explanatory microvisuals are code-native, printable, and supplementary. They
  show relationships without presenting a decorative pipeline as a system.

### Research precision

- The public question stays narrow: whether supplied origin-relation labels
  change origin-aware evidence aggregation beyond an explicit counting rule.
- F0 is descriptive; F1 is the rule-only comparator; F2 is the supplied-cue
  condition; T1 remains a separately gated descriptive transfer challenge.
- The planned primary set is `A=300`; the safety subset is provisionally fixed
  at `M=75`. These are design values, not results.
- The conservative false-corroboration endpoint now explicitly preserves its
  valid-overcount and invalid-output components.
- VOR is described as coverage of benchmark-stipulated support roots on the
  frozen multiple-origin subset, not as proof of real-world independence.
- F1/F2 parity language is now coherent: report text, order, rule, output shape,
  and resources stay matched; the relation field is the intended difference,
  so complete prompt bytes and hashes may differ while lengths and selected-
  tokenizer counts must match.
- All open gates are visible. No study, model choice, provider call,
  preregistration, external-data acquisition, spend, deployment, publication,
  or GitHub mutation is implied.
- Null, rule-only, invalidity-only, harmful, shortcut-driven, fragile,
  non-transfer, and stopped outcomes remain reportable outcomes that may narrow,
  reject, or retire the tested cue.

### Design and imagery

- The opening remains text-led.
- The recovered v13 diagram is preserved byte-for-byte as the historical
  DALL-E-created anchor and is explicitly not the current topology.
- The selected E2 derivative appears only in the deeper worked example and is
  labeled as illustration, not evidence or a dataset.
- No generated hero or new system map was used. The strongest candidate images
  were retained with a complete use/rejection table so another reviewer can
  inspect the decision rather than reconstruct it.
- The Signal Foundry case now visibly ends in `HOLD / DEFER`; it is a bounded
  translation of the framework, not independent validation.

## Plain-language research key

| Short label | Plain meaning |
| --- | --- |
| F0 | The ordinary evidence task without a special origin-counting rule. It is a descriptive baseline. |
| F1 | The same fictional reports plus an explicit rule not to count copied or derived reports as separate origin paths. |
| F2 | The same rule and reports plus supplied labels saying which paths share an origin, are separate only by the test's stipulation, or remain unresolved. |
| T1 | A possible later real-syndication transfer check. It is descriptive, rights- and annotation-gated, and never part of the main denominator. |
| A=300 / N=300 | The planned 300 primary fictional cases. This is a sample size, not a score or finding. |
| M=75 | The planned fixed 75-case multiple-origin safety subset inside the 300 cases. It remains provisional until the design gates pass. |
| FC_cons | A conservative risk measure that counts invalid answers and valid overclaims of multiple supporting origins under the benchmark rule. Its components must also be shown separately. |
| VOR | A safety check asking whether the output still uses at least two benchmark-stipulated support roots when the fixed test subset says they exist. It is not proof of real-world independence. |
| DPND / INDP / UNKN | Shared or derived path / separate only by benchmark stipulation / unresolved. `UNKN` must never be guessed into either of the first two states. |
| Negative-result commitment | The advance promise to keep and report outcomes that show no added benefit, harm, shortcut use, fragility, or non-transfer instead of changing the study until it looks successful. |

## Does this stand beyond a personal piece?

Yes, but its strongest present form and its scientific future should not be
collapsed.

| Possible output | Present assessment | Required next proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authored thought piece | Strong owner-review candidate | Owner voice/comprehension pass, several cold-reader sessions, and final copy edit. |
| Practitioner framework | Credible and useful proposal | Reusable receipt/template and evidence that other practitioners can apply it consistently. |
| Open tooling project | Strong candidate | Curated repository root, stable schema, documentation, license, examples, tests, and contribution rules. |
| Empirical paper | Serious, narrow research program; no result | Close all gates, select/freeze the model and tokenizer, register the design, run it, report every result class, and release replication artifacts. |
| Product capability | Promising design hypothesis | A bounded workflow integration measuring benefit, cost, error, and human correction behavior. |

The broad ingredients have substantial prior art. The honest opportunity is the
specific synthesis, its inspectable receipt, and the narrow controlled test of
supplied origin-relation cues beyond an explicit rule. A null result could
shrink the cue claim without erasing the conceptual argument; a harmful or
shortcut-driven result could require retiring the tested mechanism.

## Recommended owner review sequence

1. Read only the 60-90-second path. Say the idea back in your own words.
2. Continue to the four-minute stop. Decide whether the receipt and corrective
   action feel useful rather than merely clever.
3. Finish the nine-minute essay. Mark any sentence that does not sound like you
   or that feels overproduced.
4. Open three or four technical explanations, including `N=300`, `FC_cons`,
   `VOR`, and the negative-result commitment. Decide whether the visual/text
   combination teaches rather than distracts.
5. Inspect the Signal Foundry case in Explore. Confirm that `HOLD / DEFER` feels
   faithful to how you make bounded decisions.
6. Skim the Lab only after the idea itself is clear. The Lab should feel like an
   honest proposed test, not the conclusion of the thought piece.
7. Use the PDF companion for a fast visual sweep, then note any density,
   terminology, or voice concerns before requesting another build.

The models have already performed proxy cold reads and red teams. They cannot
replace the owner's judgment about voice, emotional precision, ambition, or
whether the framework expresses the intended way of thinking.
