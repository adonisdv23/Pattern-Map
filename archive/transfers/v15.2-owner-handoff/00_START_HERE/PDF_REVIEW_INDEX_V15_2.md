# Pattern Map v15.2 PDF review archive

This archive is a fast visual-comparison aid. The semantic HTML and Markdown
remain canonical.

These files are print/visual companions to the versioned arguments. They are
not pixel captures of every interactive route or exact reconstructions of
historical sites. Use the standalone HTML bundles for direct site/manuscript
review and accessible semantic structure.

## Recommended order

1. `01_THOUGHT_PIECE_V14.pdf` - the v14 print companion.
2. `02_THOUGHT_PIECE_V15.pdf` - the sealed v15 print companion.
3. `03_PATTERN_MAP_V15_1_REVIEW_COMPANION.pdf` - the sealed v15.1 convergence
   companion.
4. `04_PATTERN_MAP_V15_2_REVIEW_COMPANION.pdf` - the current 20-page owner-
   review companion.

## What to compare

- How quickly the concrete nine-reports/one-origin failure appears.
- Whether the text reads as an authored argument rather than a taxonomy or
  dashboard.
- Whether the receipt makes the changed evidence structure visible.
- Whether the research material remains visibly unrun and separate from the
  conceptual contribution.
- Whether the historical v13 image remains an origin record rather than a claim
  about current system topology.
- Whether current visuals teach a relationship without implying a truth gate,
  mandatory pipeline, or empirical result.

## Accessibility and evidence boundary

All PDFs in this archive are untagged visual/print companions. Use the current
standalone HTML or site source for semantic structure, keyboard interaction,
glossary explanations, and accessible reading. No PDF in this archive contains
an empirical result or authorizes a study, deployment, publication, provider
call, or GitHub write.
