## 3. Final thesis and opening

### Thesis

> Some evidence-sensitive AI workflows may benefit from an explicit,
> inspectable responsibility for deciding what context can influence
> generation. The responsibility should preserve source and artifact identity,
> origin relation, claim support, task relevance, authorization, cost, action,
> human correction, and later outcomes as separate records. Whether that added
> visibility improves decisions enough to justify its cost remains an empirical
> question—not a conclusion.

### Opening

> Nine favorable articles arrive through nine sites. All nine paraphrase one
> launch announcement. A summary that says “nine sources agree” has not merely
> compressed the evidence; it has changed its structure.

The correction is not “count less.” It is to count the right unit under a
stated rule, preserve the reports, and keep unresolved lineage unresolved.
Known common origin does not make a report false. Unknown origin does not make
it separate. Origin relation does not settle claim support.

Canonical argument: [`source/THOUGHT_PIECE_V15.md`](../source/THOUGHT_PIECE_V15.md).

## 7. Verified novelty boundary

### Already established or directly adjacent

- copying-aware truth discovery and source dependence;
- double-counting controls in evidence synthesis;
- citation-network amplification;
- reports-versus-underlying-study collation;
- provenance entities, activities, agents, and derivation;
- natural-language claim-provenance graphs and provenance inference;
- duplicate/reproduction clustering, including noisy copies;
- retrieval diversity, set-wise selection, redundancy evaluation, and exact
  deduplication;
- conflict/uncertainty-aware RAG and cross-source answer variation; and
- human sensitivity to dependent versus separately supported evidence.

V15 therefore cannot claim a new provenance graph, copying detector,
deduplicator, truth-discovery method, evidence unit, retrieval selector, or
general “source-aware RAG” architecture.

### Residual conceptual contribution

A boundary-preserving synthesis that keeps identity, origin relation, claim
support, permission, cost, routing, human disposition, and later outcome
records jointly inspectable without making them interchangeable.

### Residual empirical question

> With one frozen model, fixed fictional evidence, and an unchanged explicit
> rule, does a supplied typed origin-relation field change conservative origin
> counting relative to the byte-identical rule-only comparator?

This is a bounded, search-dependent residual—not a “first” claim. The closest
published natural-language comparator is Zhang, Ives, and Roth (ACL 2020),
which makes the distinction between inferred provenance and supplied oracle
relations especially important.

## 8. F0/F1/F2 protocol

| Condition | Prompt-visible difference | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F0 | Ordinary bounded evidence assessment; relation slots are `NONE` | Descriptive secondary baseline |
| F1 | Explicit origin-counting rule; relation slots remain `NONE` | Primary rule-only comparator |
| F2 | Byte-identical F1 rule plus graph-stipulated `DPND`, `INDP`, or `UNKN` | Supplied-cue condition |

### Locked confirmatory core

- Primary contrast: **F2 minus F1**.
- Primary endpoint: conservative all-assigned false corroboration (`FC_cons`).
- Primary set `A`: exactly 300 ordered, hash-locked synthetic bundles.
- Safety set `M`: exactly 75 ordered, hash-locked multiple-origin bundles,
  fixed as a subset of `A`.
- Safety gate: valid-origin recall (`VOR`) with invalid outputs coded as zero.
- Invalid outputs stay in the primary denominator as risk events.
- F1/F2 require exact evidence, order, instruction, byte, and intended-tokenizer
  parity.
- One frozen model for the first study.
- Neutral labels, order/style/surface controls, relation-noise stress, and
  explicit all-`UNKN` stress behavior.
- No calibration endpoint until a target probability and proper scoring rule
  are separately specified.

### Current stop line

The offline harness can generate and validate the full fictional corpus and
prompt receipts. It cannot authorize a run. Still required:

- owner-selected frozen model/checkpoint/tokenizer and budget;
- exact intended-tokenizer and backend chat-template parity;
- exhaustive blocked-family leakage/near-duplicate suite and independent
  semantic audit;
- independent Draft 2020-12 JSON-Schema and RFC 8785 conformance checks;
- full FC/VOR planning grid, coverage simulation, and frozen interval methods;
- independent release scan and explicit run authorization.
