## 2. Maximum current claim

V15 offers a boundary-preserving conceptual synthesis and an executable local
test harness for one narrow interface/control hypothesis:

> Preserve report-level observations without silently converting repetition
> into distinct-origin corroboration. Keep source and artifact identity,
> origin relation, claim support, authority, relevance, authorization, cost,
> action, human disposition, and later outcomes as separate records.

The remaining confirmatory question is likewise narrow:

> With one frozen model, fixed fictional evidence, and an unchanged explicit
> rule, does a supplied typed origin-relation field change conservative origin
> counting relative to the byte-identical rule-only comparator?

V15 does **not** establish provenance discovery, real-world epistemic
independence, truth, internal reasoning, human benefit, deployment value,
natural-syndication transfer, or effectiveness of the full conceptual layer.
It contains no pilot or primary result.

## 5. Research-program lock

### 5.1 Conditions and outcomes

| Condition | Prompt-visible difference | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F0 | Ordinary bounded evidence assessment; relation slots are `NONE` | Descriptive secondary baseline |
| F1 | Explicit origin-counting rule; relation slots remain `NONE` | Primary rule-only comparator |
| F2 | Byte-identical F1 rule plus stipulated `DPND`, `INDP`, or `UNKN` relation codes | Supplied-cue condition |

The primary contrast is F2 minus F1. The primary endpoint is conservative
all-assigned false corroboration (`FC_cons`). The fixed primary manifest `A`
contains exactly 300 ordered synthetic bundles. The fixed safety manifest `M`
contains exactly 75 ordered multiple-origin bundles and is a subset of `A`.
Invalid outputs remain in the primary denominator as risk events; invalid
outputs score zero in valid-origin recall (`VOR`).

The intended study requires one frozen model, exact F1/F2 evidence and rule
bytes, exact intended-tokenizer parity, identical backend chat-template
rendering except for the typed field, neutral labels, order/style/surface
controls, relation-noise stress, and explicit all-`UNKN` behavior.

### 5.2 What the offline scaffold establishes

The local standard-library implementation can:

- generate a deterministic fictional corpus;
- emit 480 total bundles, 1,920 reports, and 1,440 prompt instances;
- emit ordered/hash-locked `A=300` and `M=75` manifests;
- validate cross-record links, text and bundle hashes, graph structure, split
  membership, and F1/F2 receipt parity;
- preserve all prompt-visible codes as `UNKN` in unknown-origin stress rows;
- reject malformed model-shaped output without repair or retry;
- preserve raw bytes and recomputed raw/run receipt hashes;
- run deterministic metadata-only and surface-only diagnostics; and
- run planning-only FC and VOR simulation grids.

It performs zero model/provider calls. Its regex tokenizer is a local
development surrogate, not the intended model tokenizer. Its reduced power
command is a wiring check, not the frozen production simulation or a result.

### 5.3 Stops before any pilot or primary run

All of the following remain required:

1. explicit owner selection of a frozen model/checkpoint/tokenizer and budget;
2. exact intended-tokenizer and backend chat-template parity;
3. exhaustive blocked-family near-duplicate and leakage controls plus an
   independent semantic/stance/transformation audit;
4. independent Draft 2020-12 JSON-Schema and RFC 8785 conformance checks;
5. full FC/VOR planning grids, interval coverage simulation, and frozen
   interval methods;
6. an independent release/privacy/control-string scan;
7. any required ethics/IRB, licensing, preregistration, and data-governance
   decisions; and
8. explicit authorization for the exact run.

No current artifact satisfies or waives these gates.

## 7. Prior-art and novelty boundary

The verified record includes copying-aware truth discovery, double-counting in
evidence synthesis, citation-network amplification, claim-provenance graphs,
natural news-copy clustering, duplicate/noisy-copy detection, set-wise and
diversity retrieval, exact retrieval deduplication, conflict/uncertainty-aware
RAG, source-dependence auditing, and human sensitivity to dependent evidence.

V15 therefore does not claim to invent provenance graphs, copying detection,
deduplication, truth discovery, an evidence unit, retrieval selection, or
source-aware RAG. Its residual contribution is the bounded synthesis plus the
specified supplied-relation-cue versus rule-only test. That residual is
search-dependent, not a universal priority or “first” claim.

Every record in `research/PRIOR_ART_DELTA_V1.md` separates sourced fact from
project inference and states what the source prevents this project from
claiming.

## 14. Package integrity and extraction

Build the canonical package only from a committed payload state:

```sh
V15_PYTHON=/Users/gpt/.cache/codex-runtimes/codex-primary-runtime/dependencies/python/bin/python3
"$V15_PYTHON" tools/build_v15_package.py
"$V15_PYTHON" tools/verify_v15_package.py \
  --zip exports/DISCRIMINATION_LAYER_V15_OWNER_PACKAGE.zip
```

The builder:

- uses an explicit canonical allowlist;
- excludes dependencies, build products, cache files, intermediate QA rasters,
  stale v14 “final” surfaces, and unrelated review bundles;
- hashes every payload file;
- writes the manifest and checksum ledger;
- creates a deterministic ZIP under the top-level directory
  `Discrimination-Layer-v15/`; and
- writes the ZIP container hash to
  `exports/DISCRIMINATION_LAYER_V15_OWNER_PACKAGE.zip.sha256` because a ZIP
  cannot recursively contain its own final checksum.

Verify an extracted copy independently:

```sh

unzip -q exports/DISCRIMINATION_LAYER_V15_OWNER_PACKAGE.zip -d "$V15_EXTRACT_DIR"
```
