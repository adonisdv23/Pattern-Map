## Overall assessment

The audit found the provisional thesis intelligible, appropriately modest, and
substantially continuous with the recovered v13 concern. It also found the
research and product-case boundaries materially stronger than typical framework
prose. The remaining problems were primarily cross-artifact seams: terminology
drift, status-label overgeneralization, a few broad claims, responsive risks,
and PDF composition details.

## Material findings

1. The live v13 reference was described too strongly relative to the missing
   standalone bytes and expected hashes.
2. The five-minute distinction contract omitted standalone independence,
   operational authorization, enrichment value, and action priority.
3. The site labeled every component evidence boundary as `Prior art`, even
   where the actual status was synthesis or hypothesis.
4. C06 mixed assessment dimensions with qualifying attributes and did not align
   cleanly across JSON, Markdown, site, and PDF.
5. Enrichment value and action priority were insufficiently distinguished.
6. C02's Markdown interaction description diverged from the JSON relationship
   map.
7. Arrows on the six-family visual implied a more linear pipeline than the prose
   intended.
8. Phrases such as `nearly every`, `none new`, and `demonstrates value` exceeded
   the supported claim boundary.
9. The technical meaning of `discrimination` needed to appear adjacent to the
   title, not only later in the piece.
10. Product-case language should refer to inspected documents rather than let
    `illustrates` sound like product validation.
11. The falsifier heading was too global; a meaningful falsifier needs a named
    task class, comparator, unit, threshold, and protocol.
12. Root and site README files lagged the completed artifact state.
13. The pre-existing responsive breakpoint created a possible 681–768-pixel
    layout cliff; wide tables needed a clear horizontal-scroll affordance.
14. The PDF was untagged, split C08 awkwardly, and had uneven late-page density.
15. The final site and PDF otherwise had strong visual hierarchy and clear case,
    evidence, and counterargument boundaries.
