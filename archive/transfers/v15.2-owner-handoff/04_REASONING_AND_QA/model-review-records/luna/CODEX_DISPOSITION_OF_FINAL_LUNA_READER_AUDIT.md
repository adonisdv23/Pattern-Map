# Codex disposition of final Luna reader audit

Status: `MATERIAL_FINDINGS_INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED_AND_DISPOSITIONED`

## Accepted and applied

- Live-reference wording now states what the rendered owner-designated site
  shows while keeping exact standalone-byte comparison pending.
- The five-minute distinction contract now names independence, operational
  authorization, enrichment value, action priority, provenance, owner
  disposition, and signal-candidate boundaries.
- Site evidence-boundary labels now vary by component maturity rather than
  presenting every component as prior art.
- C06 is aligned across JSON, Markdown, site, and PDF. The assessment dimensions
  are attention priority, domain source authority, claim support, independence,
  relevance, enrichment value, action priority, and owner disposition;
  uncertainty and possible consequence are qualifying attributes.
- C02 interaction references and C07 risk wording were reconciled across the
  canonical maps.
- Linear family arrows were removed; the feedback statement remains.
- Unsupported global phrases were narrowed to targeted literature, bounded
  precedent, design propositions, or inspected-document claims.
- The title now carries the technical definition of `discrimination` directly
  beneath it.
- Both product cases explicitly identify inspected local documents and deny
  product-level validation.
- The falsifier is scoped to a named task class and requires a comparator, unit,
  threshold, and protocol.
- README state, table-scroll affordance, and the responsive breakpoint were
  corrected. Reflow was then checked at 390, 681, 720, 768, and 1440 pixels.
- PDF component starts now avoid the C08 split; glossary density was rebalanced;
  the visual-companion/accessibility boundary is stated on the cover.

## Modified rather than accepted literally

- The PDF was not retrofitted as a tagged accessible document. Independent
  inspection confirmed it is untagged, so the HTML reader is explicitly the
  canonical accessible surface and the PDF is labeled a visual/print companion.
- The relatively sparse third PDF page remains because it is not empty, clipped,
  or structurally broken and provides an intentional transition. No material
  heading is isolated.
- The navigation system was not broadly redesigned. The existing wide-screen
  rail and narrow-screen reading nav already provide the required orientation;
  the material breakpoint defect was fixed without genericizing the visual
  voice.

## Rejected or withheld

- No full visual redesign, generic dashboard treatment, or academic-paper
  restyle was adopted.
- No finding was treated as evidence of reader behavior, novelty, validation,
  enterprise readiness, or publication quality.
- No product implementation detail was promoted into the universal framework.

## Independent verification performed

- Canonical JSON parses and agrees with the Markdown and site component text.
- Site lint, build, and three server-render tests pass.
- In-app browser inspection found no document-level overflow at five tested
  widths and no runtime console errors or external resource requests.
- Refreshed desktop and mobile screenshots were visually inspected.
- The 23-page A4 PDF was regenerated, rendered page by page, reviewed as a
  contact sheet and at high resolution on representative pages, and inspected
  for page text, metadata, links, JavaScript, encryption, and tagging.
