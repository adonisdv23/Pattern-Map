# Codex disposition of early Luna audits

Model-review output is advisory analysis, not evidence. Every accepted material point below was checked against owner instructions, primary literature, standards, or exact committed local artifacts.

## Agent 1: v13 recovery and authorial intent

### Accepted

- The v13 HTML, diagram, archive, and migration packet are absent; the agent's missing-source conclusion matches bounded filesystem, attachment, Git-object, and public searches.
- The provisional thesis stated in its response accurately restates the owner authorization, but it is not represented as recovered v13 content.
- Terminology risks around “Pattern Recognition,” “discrimination,” “candidate,” “authority,” “confidence,” and “absence” are material and should be tested with readers.
- Direct conversion to an academic paper would risk losing the memo voice, visual relationships, progressive disclosure, and honest uncertainty.

### Modified

- The agent reconstructed a component hierarchy from later Alpha Solver and Signal Foundry documents. That hierarchy is useful as a hypothesis only. It must not be labeled v13 recovery or owner-originated structure until the historical inputs are inspected.
- Later repository phrases worth preserving may influence examples or case-study language, but they cannot substitute for v13 passages.

### Rejected or withheld

- No authorial attribution beyond the explicit owner authorization is accepted. The missing migration packet makes owner-versus-model provenance uncertain.
- No claim that later product documents recover the visual identity or full original reader experience is accepted.

## Agent 2: prior art and adjacent fields

### Accepted after independent verification

- The framework overlaps established work in information foraging, relevance feedback, epistemic vigilance, credibility, claim verification, structured analysis, sensemaking, provenance, evidence synthesis, mixed initiative, active learning, value of information, RAG, context management, memory, calibration, coordination, organizational learning, and decision quality.
- Cost-bounded acquisition is strongly constrained by value-of-information, metareasoning, and resource-rationality literature.
- Generic claims to invent source-aware RAG, context attribution, structured memory, reflection, or fact/belief separation are untenable given 2023–2026 primary work.
- A plausible contribution is an integrative, typed, reversible decision framework and its evaluation—not ownership of its component mechanisms.

### Modified

- Recent 2025–2026 paper claims are treated as reported results, not independently replicated truth.
- The 2026 memory-poisoning work is labeled preprint and used as a threat model, not settled evidence.
- “Decision intelligence” is treated as a broad practitioner label adjacent to established decision analysis, not a unified scientific field.

### Rejected or withheld

- Model-supplied sources that were not independently verified were not added to the canonical reference set.
- No popularity, citation-count, benchmark-superiority, or novelty assertion was adopted.

## Agent 3: framework and case-study boundaries

### Accepted after independent verification

- A coherent architecture needs an intent/authority envelope, evidence spine, provenance and claim graph, discrimination policy, human disposition, and outcome memory.
- Authority, claim support, independence, relevance, attention priority, action priority, owner disposition, and observed metadata must remain distinct.
- Source/artifact identity, common origin, typed gaps, event versus observation time, cost/stopping, privacy, override, outcome definition, and adversarial-input handling are material missing mechanisms.
- Alpha Solver's inspected discrimination packets are illustrative and unexecuted; one comparison packet explicitly reports no simulation or runtime results.
- Signal Foundry's inspected pattern and handoff documents are docs-only boundaries, not runtime or validation evidence.

### Modified

- The six-part architecture is a working synthesis for later comparison with v13, not the canonical v14 map.
- “Evidence spine” and “claim graph” are provisional explanatory terms; they will be tested against the recovered historical vocabulary.
- Enterprise controls are moved to a bounded translation document so they do not dominate the thought piece's opening.

### Rejected or withheld

- No later product taxonomy is accepted as the universal framework object model.
- No claim that Alpha Solver or Signal Foundry proves feasibility, value, completeness, or correctness is accepted.
