# Visual-system rationale

## Intended reading experience

The v14 site is designed as an editorial systems memo rather than a product
dashboard or a technical documentation portal. It should feel serious without
feeling forbidding, and structured without suggesting that the framework has
already been empirically validated.

## Reading paths

- A five-minute path introduces the problem, the provisional thesis, the
  terminology boundary, the distinction contract, the six-family map, one
  worked example, and the principal limitations.
- A complete path exposes all eleven responsibilities, their relationships,
  failure modes, cases, counterarguments, sources, and research horizon.
- A persistent section rail on wide screens and a compact horizontal reading
  nav on narrow screens make those paths visible without taking over the page.

## Visual hierarchy

- Editorial serif display type carries the argument and section titles.
- A restrained monospaced face marks statuses, identifiers, family numbers,
  and evidence boundaries.
- Warm paper tones, thin rules, and six muted family accents create structure
  without turning the memo into a software-console aesthetic.
- Large section openings establish rhythm; grids and bordered records support
  closer inspection.

## Framework representation

The system map presents six families and eleven responsibilities as a bounded
set, not as a linear pipeline. The feedback line shows that human correction
and later outcomes can revise routing without rewriting preserved evidence.
Each component expands into a consistent inspection record. The status labels
distinguish cited precedent, conceptual synthesis, illustration, and unresolved
questions.

## Accessibility and resilience intent

The page uses semantic headings, landmarks, links, lists, tables, and native
`details`/`summary` controls. A text equivalent follows the visual map. Focus is
visibly styled, reduced-motion preferences disable smooth scrolling, narrow
layouts reflow to one column, and print styles expose all component bodies. The
HTML site is the canonical accessible reading surface; the PDF is a visual and
print companion and is not represented as a tagged-PDF equivalent.

## Editorial boundary

The visual system deliberately repeats provisional, synthesis, illustrative,
and unresolved labels. Those labels are not decoration: they prevent a polished
artifact from being mistaken for validation, product proof, compliance, or a
claim of scholarly novelty. Alpha Solver and Signal Foundry appear only as
bounded examples drawn from inspected local documents.
