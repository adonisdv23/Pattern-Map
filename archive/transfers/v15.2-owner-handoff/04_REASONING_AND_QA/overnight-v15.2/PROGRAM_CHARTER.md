# Pattern Map v15.2 Candidate — Overnight Convergence Charter

## Objective

Produce a materially better owner-review candidate from the sealed v15.1
checkpoint without turning an unrun research program into a reported result,
expanding the framework merely to appear novel, or replacing authored judgment
with accumulated model output.

`v15.2 candidate` means a bounded editorial, explanatory, design, and research
hardening pass. It is not a conceptual reset and it is not a publication.

## Frozen truth boundary

- No primary or transfer study has run.
- No model has been selected for an empirical run.
- No observed effect, participant result, field outcome, or deployment result
  exists.
- The offline harness demonstrates wiring and validation behavior only.
- Benchmark-stipulated origin relations are not discovered real-world
  provenance or universal independence.
- Signal Foundry and Alpha Solver remain bounded applications, not independent
  validation.
- Null, negative, harmful, unstable, and shortcut-driven results remain
  reportable outcomes rather than failures to hide.
- No deployment, publication, GitHub push, paid provider call, participant
  recruitment, external dataset acquisition, or preregistration is authorized
  by this program.

## Reader outcome

After 60–90 seconds, a capable non-specialist should be able to say, in their
own words:

> Repetition is not automatically independent support. Before an AI writes,
> the system should preserve the judgments that decided what evidence could
> influence the answer so another person can inspect and correct them.

After about four minutes, the reader should additionally understand:

1. why nine reports may still represent one origin;
2. why origin relation, claim support, authority, relevance, permission, and a
   human decision cannot be collapsed into one score;
3. what a decision receipt preserves and what it cannot prove;
4. the human next step and the correction invariant.

After about nine minutes, the reader should additionally understand:

1. where the responsibility can live in practice, software, and model behavior;
2. when the overhead is justified and when it is not;
3. the strongest objections and retirement tests;
4. that the proposed experiment is narrow, unrun, and capable of shrinking or
   retiring the tested mechanism;
5. why the v13 image is a historical anchor rather than the current topology.

One week later, the memorable behavior should be: **count observations, preserve
unknowns, and do not silently turn repeated reports into independent support.**

## Editorial acceptance criteria

- The opening begins with a concrete failure and consequence rather than a
  framework inventory.
- The central thesis is stated decisively once, then developed rather than
  repeatedly announced.
- Main-path prose is intelligible without opening a glossary or popup.
- Technical language is introduced after an ordinary-language formulation.
- The public argument reads primarily as a serious contemporary thought piece,
  not a manifesto, research prospectus, or product requirements document.
- Caveats remain exact but do not overwhelm narrative movement.
- The manuscript names prior art precisely and makes only the bounded residual
  contribution claim the evidence supports.
- The ending gives the reader a consequence, a design test, and a falsifiable
  next step.
- No passage implies an empirical result that does not exist.

## Design and interaction acceptance criteria

- The first viewport exposes the concrete problem, the thesis, and the honest
  status without requiring internal labels.
- Reading routes are distinct, accurately timed, and do not duplicate the same
  content under different names.
- Explanatory visuals perform a named conceptual job; decorative imagery does
  not masquerade as a diagram.
- A technical-term explanation includes, only when useful: plain definition,
  why it matters here, concrete example, and what it does not mean.
- The smallest useful set of code-native microvisuals is preferred over a
  gallery. Finished-site illustrations should use semantic HTML and CSS rather
  than model-authored SVG topology.
- Popups work with keyboard, touch, screen readers, reduced motion, narrow
  screens, and print/PDF fallbacks.
- No horizontal page scroll; dense tables expose an explicit contained-scroll
  fallback and a plain-language summary.
- Existing imagery must be classified as historical anchor, explanatory
  figure, atmosphere, or rejected candidate.
- The v13 image remains unchanged and clearly historical.

## Research acceptance criteria

- Every material novelty or factual claim has a verified source/status boundary.
- Observations, inferences, and recommendations are distinguishable.
- F0/F1/F2 remain plain-language versions of one task before their short labels
  are used.
- T1 remains optional and descriptive unless a separate future decision changes
  that status.
- `N=300` / `A=300` and fixed `M=75` remain provisional design inputs, not
  results or quality scores.
- Power, denominator, invalid-output, origin-recall, tokenizer-parity, and
  shortcut gates are internally consistent and reproducible offline.
- New research opportunities are ranked by decision value, not novelty theater.
- No live run is recommended merely because scaffolding exists.

## Integration rule

Parallel agents may create lane-owned candidate files and reports. They must
not concurrently edit canonical artifacts. The parent integrator alone decides
what enters the v15.2 candidate and records each accepted, modified, deferred,
or rejected recommendation with a reason and acceptance test.

## Release gate

The candidate is owner-ready only when:

- the canonical manuscript, site, glossary, framework map, research status,
  visuals, and handoff language agree;
- focused and full local tests pass;
- the rendered reader and PDF remain legible and internally consistent;
- an independent cold reader can recover the thesis without unexplained
  shorthand;
- an adversarial methods reviewer finds no silent result, denominator, or
  novelty inflation;
- the package manifest and checksums match the files delivered;
- remaining owner decisions are few, explicit, and genuinely judgment-dependent.
