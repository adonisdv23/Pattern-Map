# Round 2 editorial selection matrix

## Pattern Recognition: The Discrimination Layer v15.1 → v15.2

**Purpose:** passage-level decisions for the parent integrator after the
independent owner / cold-reader red team.
**Scope:** the public thought piece and its route relationship to the current
v15.1 site.
**Decision vocabulary:**

- **ACCEPT** — keep the candidate passage with no substantive change;
- **REWRITE** — keep its job, but shorten, reorder, or replace wording;
- **DELETE** — remove it from the public essay; preserve its evidence or route
  elsewhere when noted;
- **RESTORE** — bring back a bounded v15.1 passage / record / capability in the
  specified deeper route, not necessarily into the main essay.

The candidate is the preferred base. “Restore” does not mean revert to v15.1;
it means recover a specific loss that protects memory, evidence status, or
practitioner usefulness.

## Final route contract to integrate

Use the following promise verbatim or with only punctuation changes:

> **60–90 seconds:** see the nine-report failure, the `09 / 01 / 00 / HOLD`
> receipt, and why zero counted support is a hold—not a rejection.
> **About five minutes:** understand the three questions, the correction
> invariant, the human next step, and that no study has run.
> **12–15 minutes:** inspect the loops, use boundary, objections, and the
> narrow research bridge.
> **Optional deeper routes:** Explore holds the six-family records and cases;
> Lab holds the protocol gates; Sources holds prior-art status and history.

The route labels must point to actual stop markers. A route is not “five
minutes” merely because its first five minutes contain the first heading.

## Terminology / popup shortlist for integration

The manuscript must stand without interaction. The site may add one
plain-language explanation at the first high-value occurrence of each item.

| Term | Visible first wording | Popup content required | Popup boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `origin` | “where a report’s information came from” | Nine reports trace to one announcement; origin is a relation to inspect. | Not truth, authority, permission, or real-world independence. |
| `shared path` / `separate only in this test` / `unresolved` | Use these three labels first. | One receipt-grounded example for each state. | “Separate in this test” is stipulated, not discovered provenance. |
| `claim support` | “what exact claim this passage supports, refutes, or leaves unsettled” | Vendor announcement versus field-reliability claim. | Not source popularity or origin count. |
| `provenance` | After the plain explanation, “a trace of origin, transformation, custody, and time” | Nine-report derivation example. | Does not prove correctness, authority, permission, or independence. |
| `recorded human decision` / `disposition` | Prefer “recorded human next step.” | Hold / verify / proceed example. | Not truth and not an automatic action. |
| `discrimination layer` | Title only; body uses “pre-answer responsibility.” | Technical differentiation among information and possible actions. | Not a social classifier or a universal score; rename if readers persist in the wrong thesis. |
| `origin-relation field` | “a supplied note about whether reports share a path” | Lab condition example. | Does not discover real provenance. |
| `offline harness` | “local test machinery; no model was called” | Lab status only; no visual needed. | Not an experiment or deployment. |
| `300 planned cases` | “one provisional plan for 300 fictional cases” | Lab sample / denominator explanation. | Not participants, results, confidence, or quality. |
| `tokenizer`, `denominator`, `leakage`, `safety set` | Do not show in the public five-minute route. | Define in Lab only if retained. | Methods gates are not evidence that a study ran or was authorized. |

Every popup must include a plain definition, one concrete example, and a
boundary; it must work from keyboard / touch / assistive technology, close
explicitly, preserve position, and remain nonessential when unavailable. These
are integration acceptance conditions, not claims that the current site has
been tested in this Round 2 review.

## Selection bottom line

The candidate earns the base slot because it gives the owner a usable receipt,
a consequential correction rule, a bounded “when to use it” answer, and an
honest research boundary in substantially less space. The accepted v15.2 form
should be a thought piece with a receipt and a small design test. The component
map, source-status ledger, product translations, and experimental gates should
remain available as deeper records. The red-team recommendation is therefore:

**Accept the candidate’s argument and voice; rewrite the route; delete the
packet metadata; restore only the evidence and implementation details that make
the receipt inspectable; and stop before the piece becomes another inventory.**
