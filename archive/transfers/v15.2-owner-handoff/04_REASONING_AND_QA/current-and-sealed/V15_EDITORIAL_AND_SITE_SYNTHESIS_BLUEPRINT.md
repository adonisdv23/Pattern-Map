# V15 editorial and site synthesis blueprint

- **Program:** Pattern Recognition / The Discrimination Layer v15
- **Prepared:** 2026-08-18
- **Status:** canonical integration blueprint; all three initial research lanes
  have received recorded dispositions
- **Empirical status:** no model study, pilot, participant study, transfer run,
  or field evaluation has occurred

## Answer first

V15 should be an argument a skeptical reader can follow without opening a
single control: repeated reports can share an origin, so a system that counts
reports may manufacture corroboration before generation begins. The proposed
response is not a truth machine. It is an inspectable responsibility for
keeping origin relation, claim support, authority, relevance, permission,
action priority, and owner disposition separate while deciding what context
may influence an answer.

The reader should encounter that argument in this order:

1. a concrete false-corroboration failure;
2. the narrow thesis and its limits;
3. one typed, corrigible origin-accounting receipt;
4. the larger six-family, eleven-responsibility system;
5. the strongest objections and retirement tests;
6. a visually separate research program labeled **no results**.

The site has two conceptual reading depths—**Essay** and **Explore**—and one
separate empirical-status track—**Lab**. It is a long-form reading experience,
not an operational dashboard. The PDF is a curated print companion, not a
screenshot of the interface. The HTML remains the canonical accessible
surface because the planned PDF is untagged.

## 1. Canonical claim ladder

| Level | V15 may say | V15 may not say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observed failure pattern | Repeated reports may share one source path, so report count need not equal distinct-origin support under a stated relation rule. | Copies are false, recurrence is worthless, or public disagreement proves independence. |
| Conceptual proposal | Make pre-generation evidence judgments typed, inspectable, correctable, and bounded by permission and cost. | The framework discovers truth, provenance, authority, or the correct action automatically. |
| Synthesis contribution | The project integrates mature ideas behind a boundary-preserving evidence-selection and action responsibility. | The project invented provenance, copying detection, truth discovery, evidence synthesis, RAG, or human review. |
| Testable hypothesis | In one frozen model on synthetic graphs, supplied typed origin relations may reduce conservative false corroboration beyond an explicit rule. | F2 works, reveals internal reasoning, transfers to people or deployments, or validates all eleven responsibilities. |
| Transfer boundary | NEWS-COPY and Newswire may support a separately named descriptive T1 after rights and annotation gates. | T1 is F3, public nonduplicates are `INDP`, or transfer rows belong in `A` or `M`. |
| Empirical status | The protocol and deterministic offline harness are implementation-ready subject to named owner gates. | Results, effect estimates, publication status, preregistration, or deployment readiness. |

All canonical artifacts use the status vocabulary in
`research/V15_CLAIM_SOURCE_ARTIFACT_MATRIX.md`. Every empirical-looking panel
must include both the evidence status and the strongest prohibited inference.

## 2. Editorial architecture

### 2.1 Title and opening

Keep the thought-piece title distinct from the scientific-paper title:

- thought piece: **Pattern Recognition: The Discrimination Layer**;
- subtitle: **Why repeated reports can still amount to one origin—and what an
  AI system should preserve before it generates**;
- first-paper working title: **Oracle Origin-Relation Metadata in One Frozen
  Model: A Controlled False-Corroboration Benchmark**.

The first 250 words open inside the worked failure. Do not begin with six
families, eleven responsibilities, a glossary, “AI is changing everything,” or
a claim that context quality matters. The opening should make one counted
error visible:

> Nine favorable reports arrive through different sites. All nine paraphrase
> one launch announcement. A summary that says “nine sources agree” has not
> merely compressed the evidence; it has changed its structure.

The next move is the thesis, not a taxonomy: preserve each report as an
observation, but do not silently convert recurrence into distinct-origin
support. Unknown origin remains unknown. A known common origin does not make a
claim false. Origin relation does not settle stance, authority, truth,
permission, or action.

### 2.2 Essay sequence

The canonical manuscript uses this spine:

1. **Nine reports, one origin.** Concrete opening and the exact counting error.
2. **The judgment before the answer.** Consequential choices often happen in
   acquisition, grouping, selection, exclusion, and stopping.
3. **The receipt.** A complete text-readable worked example with nine dependent
   observations, two separate illustrative roots whose stance is unassessed,
   zero counted supporting origins for the broad-validation claim, and a human
   `HOLD` disposition.
4. **What must remain separate.** Explain, in prose and one compact table,
   source versus artifact; recurrence versus origin relation; origin relation
   versus claim support; authority versus truth; relevance versus action;
   access versus authorization; observation versus decision versus outcome.
5. **A system responsibility, not another oracle.** Introduce the six families
   and eleven responsibilities as a revisable decomposition. Keep the prose
   concise; detailed records belong in Explore.
6. **Two loops and one preserved history.** A fast evidence/action loop and a
   slower outcome/policy loop, with no silent update and no erased receipt.
7. **When the overhead earns its place.** Consequential, contested,
   dependence-heavy, changing, or permission-sensitive work; not simple
   extraction, calculation, or low-stakes rewriting.
8. **How the idea could fail.** Old-work/new-label, gatekeeping, rigor theater,
   excess cost, decorative review, contaminated feedback, construct collapse,
   and confusing terminology.
9. **What is actually novel enough to test.** Only the residual contribution
   that survives the verified prior-art delta.
10. **A research program that may return zero.** F0/F1/F2, conservative
    endpoint, fixed safety set, shortcut tests, descriptive T1, and the locked
    negative-result commitment.
11. **Close on correction.** The aim is not to automate certainty; it is to
    make a consequential evidence judgment visible early enough to contest.

### 2.3 Copy rules

- Prefer paragraphs with one causal claim over repeated cards with three
  labels and a slogan.
- Keep definitions close to the sentence that needs them. The glossary is a
  reference, not the opening burden.
- Use “may,” “proposes,” “under the stated rule,” “benchmark-stipulated,” and
  “tested frozen model” when those qualifications carry real meaning. Remove
  ornamental hedging.
- Never use “first,” “nobody,” “solved,” “complete provenance,” “independent
  sources,” or “ground truth” without the synthetic-benchmark qualifier.
- Give each limitation once in the argument, then collect only the concise
  audit list near the end.
- Avoid generic transitions such as “in an era of,” “at its core,” “the key
  insight,” “not just X but Y,” and “this is where.”
- Use code-like labels (`DPND`, `INDP`, `UNKN`, `NONE`, F0/F1/F2, T1) only in
  the receipt or Lab. The essay uses ordinary language first.
- Treat “discrimination” as technical differentiation among information and
  possible actions; preserve the rename test if readers still infer a
  social-classification or classifier thesis.

## 3. Worked example and cross-artifact count contract

The same fictional receipt appears in manuscript, site, PDF, and tests:

| Field | Canonical value |
| --- | --- |
| Receipt | `ORIGIN-EX-01`, fictional, no live data |
| Decision | Whether a sandbox pilot of a data-migration tool is warranted |
| Permission | Sandbox only; no production data; 90-minute research budget |
| Claim | “The tool is broadly validated.” |
| Observations | `O01`–`O09`, nine unordered report observations |
| Known relation | All nine derive from `Origin A`, one launch announcement |
| Relation state | `DEPENDENT` for `O01`–`O09` under the example’s declared rule |
| Known common-origin clusters | `1` |
| Counted supporting origins for the broad-validation claim | `0` because the launch announcement’s support for that broad claim is not established |
| Contrast roots | `B1` and `C1`, separately rooted only as an illustration; claim support unassessed |
| Unknown rule | Never move an unresolved relation into dependent or independent totals |
| Claim state | `INSUFFICIENT` |
| Human disposition | `HOLD · VERIFY ANOTHER ORIGIN RELATION` |

The count of zero is intentional: a known launch-announcement origin does not
automatically support the broader claim that the tool is validated. This
prevents origin count from laundering claim stance. A short note must explain
that decision anywhere the count is displayed.

E2, `nine-mentions-one-origin.jpg`, remains an illustration with a visible
“no result” boundary and a complete text equivalent. The v13 map remains
byte-identical historical evidence with its supplied hash. H1 remains archived.

## 4. Site information architecture

### 4.1 Global frame

The top-level navigation communicates epistemic status before section count:

- **Essay** — the authored argument; default path, approximately 8 minutes;
- **Explore** — framework map, eleven expandable records, typed receipt,
  counterarguments, glossary, and references;
- **Lab · no results** — the scientific question, frozen core, implementation
  readiness, descriptive T1 decision, and locked result commitment.

Use same-page anchors in one server-rendered document. JavaScript may improve
orientation and disclosure behavior, but no claim, count, status, citation, or
navigation target depends on client hydration.

### 4.2 First fold

The first fold contains only:

- a small local-review / v15 status line;
- the title and concrete subtitle;
- the nine-reports/one-origin opening;
- the narrow proposition;
- three text links: read the essay, inspect the receipt, visit the lab.

Do not place the complete receipt, a large route-card grid, or a dashboard-like
status rail above the fold on mobile. At 390 × 844, the reader should see the
problem and at least one clear next action without scrolling through a full
screen of metadata.

### 4.3 Essay

Use a quiet editorial column, strong typographic hierarchy, wide margins, and
occasional pull quotations. Retain the warm paper ground, dark ink, restrained
teal, and coral risk accent from v14. Reduce boxed surfaces. A box is justified
only when it changes reading mode: receipt, historical artifact, explicit
counterargument, or Lab boundary.

### 4.4 Explore

The framework map remains deterministic HTML. Six family rows show their
question, output, and component IDs. Eleven native `<details>` elements carry
the complete responsibility records. Each summary must expose ID, name,
one-sentence purpose, and evidence status before expansion.

The relationship view must show:

- no required linear pipeline;
- fast evidence/action loop;
- slower outcome/policy proposal loop;
- human disposition and permission at consequential boundaries;
- append-only history and explicit supersession;
- uncertainty that can route backward without being relabeled.

The recovered v13 raster is introduced as a historical origin map beside an
HTML transcription of its six labels. It must not be presented as current
topology.

### 4.5 Lab

The Lab begins after a high-contrast horizontal threshold and repeats:

> Research program · protocol and offline harness · no model run · no results

It includes:

- the exact F0/F1/F2 table;
- primary contrast F2 minus F1;
- conservative all-assigned false corroboration endpoint;
- fixed-set supporting-origin recall gate;
- one frozen model and exact F1/F2 parity;
- neutral-code, order, style, surface-leakage, metadata-only, field-only, and
  relation-noise checks;
- descriptive T1 disposition, outside `A`, `M`, confidence intervals, tests,
  and VOR;
- four outcome interpretations: positive and safe; null/rule-only; harmful or
  blanket discounting; shortcut-driven or unstable;
- links to the prospectus, protocol, readiness audit, prior-art delta, and
  transfer memo.

There is no empty chart, mock result card, effect-size placeholder, “coming
soon” performance number, or visual that resembles a completed study.

## 5. Visual system

### 5.1 Direction

The intended feeling is a carefully edited field memo: warm, exact, and
slightly forensic. Use typography and whitespace to create pacing. The
interface should feel authored, not generated from a component gallery.

- body: highly legible serif with a conservative system fallback;
- interface/meta: compact sans-serif;
- numbers/codes: tabular figures and monospaced face only where precision
  matters;
- color: paper, ink, muted teal, mineral green, restrained coral, soft violet;
- rules: hairline dividers and bands instead of repeated floating cards;
- motion: none required; any enhancement must disappear under
  `prefers-reduced-motion` without loss.

### 5.2 Visual truth rules

- Arrow means a named relationship or possible route, never maturity or
  inevitability.
- Color never carries evidence status alone.
- Size never implies evidence strength unless explicitly labeled.
- The eleven responsibilities have no maturity ranking.
- The receipt is a record, not a workflow diagram.
- T1 sits laterally outside the confirmatory study, never as a fourth arm.
- Decorative imagery cannot carry exact topology, counts, status, or citations.

## 9. Acceptance test

A thoughtful skeptical reader should be able to restate all five points after
the Essay path:

1. The visible error is false corroboration from report-level repetition.
2. Known common origin does not make a report false, and unknown does not mean
   independent.
3. The proposal is an inspectable pre-generation responsibility, not a truth
   oracle or mandatory product box.
4. Adjacent fields already contain the component mechanisms; the residual
   contribution is a bounded synthesis and an explicit cue-use test.
5. No empirical result exists; the research design is allowed to return null,
   harmful, unstable, or shortcut-driven evidence.

If the page communicates fewer than five, the editorial loop remains open.
If it communicates more certainty than these five, the epistemic-status loop
has failed.
