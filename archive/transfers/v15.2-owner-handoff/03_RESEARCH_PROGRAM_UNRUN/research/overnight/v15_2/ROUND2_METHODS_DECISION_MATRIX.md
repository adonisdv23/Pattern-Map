## Decision legend

- **ACCEPT** — retain the Round 1 recommendation as stated or with the
  explicitly bounded clarification below. “Accepted gate” means the gate is
  still open until its receipt exists.
- **REVISE** — retain the objective but change the endpoint, wording, or
  acceptance contract before lock.
- **DEFER** — keep as future work or a future comparator outside v1; do not
  imply that it has been completed or is needed for the current confirmatory
  family.
- **REJECT** — do not carry the proposed broad claim or action forward.
