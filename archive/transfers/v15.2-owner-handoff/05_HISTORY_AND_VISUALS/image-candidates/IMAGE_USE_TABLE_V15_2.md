# v15.2 generated-design, historical-anchor, preview, and production-raster inventory

**Status:** local owner-review candidate; no publication or deployment
authorized  
**Purpose:** account for every generated design image, recovered historical
anchor, image-generation preview, and production raster eligible for the
v15.2 deliverables. The earlier scoring rationale remains in
`IMAGE_SELECTION_LEDGER.md`.

## Provenance boundary

- The clean candidate files were produced through OpenAI image generation. The
  interface did not expose an exact model name, so this package does not infer
  one.
- The six `chatgpt-images/previews/` files are screenshots from the signed-in
  ChatGPT Images comparison. They contain interface chrome and are audit
  evidence only.
- The v13 map is the unchanged historical DALL-E-created anchor. It is not a
  v15.2 topology, generated finding, or current framework diagram.
- V15.2 deliberately uses CSS-native semantic microvisuals for its public
  receipt, trace/action distinction, and planned-condition comparison. No
  generated raster defines a current component, sequence, relation, truth
  state, or empirical result.

## Deliberately excluded QA and review rasters

Raw screenshots under `reports/qa/`, `reviews/claude_desktop/packet/`, and
similar historical review folders document former browser states or review
interfaces. They are not design candidates or production assets, many predate
v15.2, and the owner archive intentionally excludes them. Their material
findings and dispositions are preserved in the included Markdown/JSON review
records. Temporary PDF page renders are likewise excluded after visual QA.
