# V13-to-v16 fidelity matrix

Status: **BINDING CONTINUITY CHECK**

This matrix protects the broad original intent while accepting later rigor. It
uses recovered v13 material for the idea and six families; v14/v15 for caution,
operational distinctions, accessibility, and design lessons; and v15.2 only as
the source checkpoint for The Echo Problem and selectively reusable patterns.

## Intentional departures from v13

V16 does not preserve unsupported or overbroad formulations merely because they
are historical. It must not claim:

- that useful answers generally sit at the periphery;
- that different ecosystems are automatically independent;
- that source track record determines truth;
- that non-experts can produce expert-grade judgment;
- that every AI team needs the complete layer;
- that one learning ledger can update reliable weights automatically;
- that a custom model is the deepest or most defensible implementation; or
- that the historical seven steps are one mandatory linear pipeline.

These are claim and implementation corrections, not permission to remove the
reader problem, practical ambition, or any of the six families.

## Guard against the v15.2 drift

Origin accounting belongs primarily to source weighing and structured source
relationships. It may also illustrate why memory and comparison matter. It
must not consume the opening, replace peripheral signal as a core intuition,
erase velocity or absence, turn the learning loop into protocol mechanics, or
make the six-family map appear to be a provenance system.

The decisive test is removal: if the nine-reports/one-announcement example is
removed, the 90-second version, full essay, six-family map, builder framework,
and agent procedure must still express a coherent and useful broad idea.
