# Two-project separation

Status: **PERMANENT OWNER DECISION**

## Project A — Pattern Recognition / The Discrimination Layer v16

V16 is the principal broad work. It begins from the recovered v13 reader
problem and six families, incorporates later rigor and design lessons, and
produces a human thought piece, builder framework, agent playbook, local
interactive reader, and future research agenda.

Its question is broad: how can people and AI systems make better, more
inspectable choices about what to notice, compare, preserve, question, and
allow to influence generation?

## Project B — The Echo Problem / ECHO-01

**The Echo Problem: When Repetition Masquerades as Corroboration** is a
standalone thought piece, origin-accounting framework, and unrun controlled
research protocol. V15.2 is its exact source checkpoint. The curated successor
starts at EP v0.1; historical v15.2 files retain their original names and
status.

The Echo Problem must preserve:

- the v15.2 manuscript and site;
- protocol, offline harness, fixtures, tests, and prior art;
- the explicit statement that no model or participant study has run;
- null, rule-only, invalidity-driven, harmful, shortcut-driven,
  surface/semantic-audit-failure, unstable, noise-fragile, non-transfer, and
  stopped or quarantined outcome classes; and
- its own EP version history and future low/no-cost execution plan.

## Allowed relationship

V16 may:

- use common-origin recurrence as one worked example;
- reuse carefully reviewed interface or receipt patterns;
- point readers to The Echo Problem for deeper origin-accounting treatment; and
- identify ECHO-01 as Research Track/Paper 01 candidate.

The Echo Problem may:

- explain that origin accounting is one mechanism within the broader Pattern
  Recognition family system; and
- link back to v16 for the larger philosophy and operating playbook.

## Prohibited collapse

Neither project may:

- rename historical v15.2 files as if they always belonged to The Echo Problem;
- present the Echo protocol as validation of v16;
- present v16's six families as validated by a future Echo result;
- make nine-reports/one-announcement the definition or required opening of v16;
- import F0/F1/F2 notation into the human v16 essay as required knowledge; or
- imply that a protocol, fixture, static test, or model review is an empirical
  result.

## File and version boundaries

- V16: `manuscript/**`, `framework/**`, `cases/**`, `site/**`, and the broad
  research agenda.
- Echo: `research/the-echo-problem/**` and the exact v15.2 transfer accession at
  `archive/transfers/v15.2-owner-handoff/**`.
- History: `archive/v15.2/**` and the transfer accession preserve the original
  checkpoint; EP v0.1 is a new curated successor, not an in-place rewrite.

## Separation test

The boundary passes only if both statements are true:

1. Remove all Echo-specific examples and notation: v16 still explains a broad,
   six-family, human-first discipline and operational agent workflow.
2. Remove all v16 claims beyond origin accounting: The Echo Problem still
   stands as a complete, honest, no-results thought piece and research program.
