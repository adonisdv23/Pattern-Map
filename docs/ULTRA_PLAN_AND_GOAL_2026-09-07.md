## What changes in the plan

Use parallel work to challenge the mechanism, design cases that could defeat
it, and inspect a concrete portfolio integration. Use cross-review after each
bounded authoring task. Do not treat more agents, more text or agreement as
stronger evidence. Preserve the current human essay and concentrate effort on
questions where a different finding could change our next action.

The leading research question is whether explicit transfer obligations and
selective evidence acquisition improve a decision under a limited budget.
Careful ordinary work and explicit purpose/mechanism comparison are both strong
baselines. A simpler procedure winning is a useful result. Timing alone may
change nothing when every candidate is reconsiderable and all relevant facts
and checks are already available; the design must identify the actual resource,
information or commitment constraint.

Keep observation/capture work as a narrower measurement question. Its original
candidate packets need stronger diversity, visible source IDs, separate claims
and actions, and explicit costs. They are not independent approved study tasks.
Defer correction-memory evaluation until changing applicability and repeated
practical need justify a comparison with existing memory methods.
