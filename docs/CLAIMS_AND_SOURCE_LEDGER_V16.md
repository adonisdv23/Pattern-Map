# V16 claims and source ledger

Status: **CURRENT CLAIM-CONTROL RECORD / NOT A LITERATURE REVIEW / NOT RESULTS**

This ledger records what the active v16 artifacts may say, the strongest basis
currently available for saying it, and the upgrade that is not allowed. It is
not a score of truth and does not turn owner intent, a historical artifact, a
validator, or an advisory review into empirical evidence.

The source authority order in `SOURCE_AUTHORITY_AND_LINEAGE.md` governs when
records conflict. The optional public route in
`manuscript/SOURCES_AND_RESEARCH_ROUTE.md` remains targeted rather than
exhaustive. External links must be rechecked before any later-authorized public
release.

| `REJECTED_OR_NARROWED` | A historical or proposed formulation the current project must not repeat | A claim that the opposite has been empirically proven |

## Rejected or narrowed formulations

The following formulations are not allowed in current v16 public copy unless a
later exact owner instruction and adequate evidence change the record:

- the useful answer generally lives at the periphery;
- multiple publishers or URLs are independent corroboration;
- provenance, source reputation, or a trust score establishes correctness;
- velocity or expected absence can be inferred without a baseline;
- the six families are newly invented or universally complete;
- the Discrimination Layer is one required software component or model
  architecture;
- a custom model is inherently the deepest or most defensible path;
- the playbook, Signal Foundry case, Echo harness, validators, advisory audits,
  or polished site prove that the framework works;
- non-experts or agents can replace expert judgment; or
- an unrun protocol, fixture, power scaffold, or future outcome category is a
  research result.
- same-original news reproduction labels establish claim support, truth, or
  independence; different reproduction clusters are automatically independent;
- every measured origin-rate estimate is necessarily a lower bound; or
- nobody has measured retrieval redundancy/dependence and the measurement
  paper therefore has an uncontested novelty claim.
- Pattern Map, the Discrimination Layer, or its six-family arrangement is the
  first, only, or proven way to govern pre-generation context choices;
- context engineering, adaptive retrieval/stopping, memory, attribution,
  source diversity, or correction was invented by this project; or
- a citation, influence estimate, retrieved-document attribution, benchmark
  label, or accepted human correction establishes truth by itself.

## Maintenance rule

Every material new public claim must receive an ID, strongest actual status,
canonical basis, and prohibited upgrade before integration. A source may
constrain or contextualize a claim without validating the six-family
arrangement. Research-driven edits may narrow a claim; they may not silently
replace the owner-approved thesis. Later evidence changes receive a new ledger
entry or explicit revision record rather than retroactive inflation of the
current basis.
