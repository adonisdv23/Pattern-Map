## Changes completed

### Current evidence formats

Forty-nine tracked files in current `qa/visual/` evidence were named `.png`
although their first bytes identified JPEG data. Each was renamed with `git mv`
to its truthful `.jpg` extension. File bytes were not converted. Active
non-archive references were updated to the renamed path; the underlying QA
findings and capture dates were not rewritten.

`qa/visual/verify_image_formats.py` now checks PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WebP magic
signatures deterministically. It fails on a current mismatch and reports
immutable mismatches separately. The check found zero current mismatches.

The checker reports 40 retained mismatches under the immutable
`archive/transfers/v14-complete-2026-08-18/` transfer. No file under `archive/`
or `research/` was edited, renamed, or rehashed.

### Stylesheet hygiene and map safety

The generated v16 site no longer renders the earlier topology-coordinate
system. The old absolute-position map rules, detachable connector styling,
unused map labels/output/learning rules, unused route-receipt styling, and
other unreachable selectors were removed from `site/src/site.css`. The harmful
old whole-route `.map-route` border/background rule was also removed. The
current map remains a normal-flow, line-free relationship view whose meaning is
carried by its text and relationship bands.

`qa/site/css-selector-use.spec.mjs` now checks generated routed pages and the
standalone export for stylesheet selectors that have no rendered or runtime
owner. It also checks that current map geometry cannot regain absolute
positioning or non-auto fixed coordinates. The existing
`qa/interaction/map-layout-contract.spec.mjs` carries the same regression gate.

The standalone export was regenerated by the documented site build. The
ReportLab PDF companion is generated independently of `site/src/site.css`, so
the stylesheet change does not affect its source or layout. A local regeneration
attempt was blocked because this worktree does not have the optional `reportlab`
module installed; the committed PDF SHA-256 remained unchanged, and no fresh
PDF render or screenshot is claimed here.
