## Blunt verdict

The reviewer found the broad thesis, six-family continuity, human-authority
boundary, three principal doors, warm visual language, source-driven build,
and Echo/v16 separation worth preserving. It did not recommend a wholesale
redesign.

It identified two source-deterministic P0 defects:

1. The Apply studio turned planning selections into event-like states—such as
   completion, stopped status, learning state, and human disposition—that had
   not occurred. Restricted permission also did not reliably dominate route
   shortcuts.
2. The Map used conflicting breakpoint rules: the 821–1100-pixel layout moved
   to three family columns and two record rows, while a later 821-pixel rule
   restored desktop absolute positions. That could deterministically overlap
   or detach visual elements at common widths.

The reviewer also found P1 friction in reading continuity, terminology,
no-script Apply behavior, map sequencing, the density of the cumulative short
version, late examples, and the playbook's bureaucracy risk.

## Findings accepted into the correction pass

### Apply integrity and permission precedence — P0

The interactive surface must produce only a recommendation about future work:
recommended level, recommended action, required gate, planned stopping
condition, and optional learning path. It must not manufacture a completed
run, triggered stop, observed outcome, learning review, or human decision.

Restricted permission must yield `CLARIFY`; a required human gate must yield
`HOLD`, regardless of the selected consequence, uncertainty, or budget. Any
demonstration of later state must be explicitly labeled a local simulation and
must leave observed fields unchanged.

### Responsive relationship semantics — P0/P1

The current visual should teach a small number of truthful relationships, not
look like a mandatory pipeline. Baseline, common origin, human authority, and
conditional learning should be explicit in text. A line-free, flow-native map
is preferable here to fragile SVG endpoints: the relationship survives resize,
print, narrow screens, and color loss without pretending that every family maps
to one record or one next step.

The focus panel should retain six separate fields: Question, Inputs,
Comparison, Record, Boundary, and Connections. The open text equivalent should
remain canonical for meaning.

### Human reading path and terminology — P1

The cumulative entry should place one concrete example before the full family
enumeration, delay the internal framework name until the function is
understood, and stay closer to an ordinary 60–90-second reading load.

The routed site should remain, but it should offer a generated continuous
Guided mode composed from the same canonical sources and components. This mode
must not fork a second manuscript or replace Read / Map / Apply.

Difficult terms should use an inline-first contract. Essential meaning remains
visible in the sentence; an optional click/touch/keyboard explainer may add a
code-native microvisual, boundary, and glossary link. Meaning must never exist
only on hover.

### Progressive enhancement and proportion — P1

Without JavaScript, the Apply form should not appear actionable. A complete
static decision guide should be the readable canonical fallback.

The agent playbook should begin with Stage 0: determine whether the task
actually contains an evidence-selection decision. A reversible transformation
of supplied material should bypass evidence registers, comparison matrices,
and learning plans unless the task genuinely introduces acquisition,
comparison, uncertainty, or consequential influence.

### Interaction and accessibility corrections — P2

- Announce one concise Apply status rather than marking the entire card live.
- Guard `IntersectionObserver` so enhancement failure is nonfatal.
- Keep discrete controls at the project's 44-by-44-pixel target without
  inflating ordinary inline links.
- Preserve visible first-use definitions for print and no-script output.
- Prefer the text relationship equivalent in print.
- Keep generated bitmap imagery at zero for this pass; the identified needs
  are semantic and relational, and code-native visuals are more responsive,
  printable, and accessible.

## Findings not accepted as canonical changes

The reviewer suggested considering a softer headline than “AI slop often
begins before the model writes a word.” That wording was not changed. It is the
owner-approved editorial center, was already reviewed under D-013/PAOB-03, and
is presented as a conceptual premise rather than a measured prevalence or
causal claim.

The reviewer also raised lower-priority possibilities such as URL-persisted
family focus, broader card-shape/rhythm revisions, and terminology replacement
across every specialist appendix. These remain optional polish or later
bounded improvements; they are not allowed to silently rewrite the framework,
historical family identifiers, or technical artifacts.
