## Integrated outcomes and dispositions

### 1. Ordinary work remains ordinary

**Accepted with revision.** Stage 0 is terminal inside the copyable operating
brief. When no evidence-selection workflow is needed, the receipt contains
exactly supplied scope, assumptions, unchecked boundaries, and output. It does
not create evidence, comparison, disconfirmation, influence, outcome, or
learning bureaucracy.

### 2. Layered receipts fail closed without pretending to establish truth

**Accepted with revision.** Permission uses the distinct states `AUTHORIZED`,
`UNKNOWN`, `NOT_AUTHORIZED`, and `REVOKED`. A globally blocked receipt has empty
downstream collections. Comparison and disconfirmation claims resolve to
substantive linked records or bounded typed exceptions. Motion requires at
least two distinct authorized observations at two valid, distinct UTC instants
under one alignment key. Only accepted `CURRENT` and `AUTHORIZED` memory may
influence a later answer, and the synthetic correction fixture preserves one
linear hash-bound lineage.

These checks establish schema and validator behavior only. They do not prove
that a source is true, that permission is legally sufficient, that a runtime
store is immutable, that an agent complies, or that an answer improves.

### 3. Public presentation is an adapter over the same source

**Accepted with revision.** Review and public modes retain the same ten route
IDs, forty-four canonical source hashes, claim anchors, and F1–F6 tuple. Public
mode removes owner/package/repository chrome from the normal reading flow and
places the real short-version prose in the first Read viewport. One fixed
four-stage teaching reveal replaces its prior state rather than accumulating
claims. It has a static/no-script/print equivalent and makes no network call,
model call, score, automated decision, or observed-result claim.

Release metadata fails closed unless an explicit release build is paired with
valid absolute-HTTPS identity fields. Canonical bases reject credentials,
whitespace, queries, and fragments; route URLs are resolved with URL semantics
and preserve a valid project subpath. Ordinary public previews remain
`noindex,nofollow`. No byline, canonical public URL, social image, public link
recheck, deployment, or publication was selected.

### 4. Current research constrains the claims without taking over the thesis

**Accepted with revision.** The optional source route adds a targeted,
non-exhaustive 2025–2026 primary-source check. It supports a design/governance
synthesis and testable-agenda ceiling, not novelty or validation of a new
technical mechanism. The narrow-wedge memo separates generation studies from
fixed-answer interface studies: answer accuracy may be eligible only when an
arm actually generates an answer, while correction, acceptance, and burden are
the eligible outcomes for a frozen-answer interface comparison.

Observation, capture/process, access, permission, and currency are represented
as different axes. The initial unrun wedge holds access `AVAILABLE`, permission
`AUTHORIZED`, and currency `CURRENT` while varying observation and capture.
No first paper, provider, model, corpus, sample, study, or spend was selected.
