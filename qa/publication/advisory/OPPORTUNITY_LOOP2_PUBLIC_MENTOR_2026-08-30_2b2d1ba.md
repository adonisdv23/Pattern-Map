## Cold-reader and public-surface assessment

### First contact and breadth

The public Home still opens with the human problem:

> AI slop often begins before the model writes a word.

The standfirst explains polished-but-generic output through upstream search,
specialist perspective, comparison, expected absence, and prior context. The
three principal doors—`Read the idea`, `Explore the map`, and `Apply it`—come
before the deterministic teaching reveal. The public reader can enter the
human idea without seeing a protocol, release checklist, source manifest, or
agent starter.

The public Map, Home family strip, Guided route, and Read prose retain all six
families in their locked order: peripheral signal, source weighing,
velocity/motion, absence + memory, structured patterns, and the learning loop.
The Map explicitly says there is no required starting order. The three example
patterns teach different distinctions rather than replacing the six-family
map. This is proportionate breadth, not a claim that all six are mandatory in
every task.

**Disposition: Accepted.** No P0, P1, or P2 was found in first contact,
all-six-family breadth, or the human-first order. A fresh owner/mentor
restatement remains a manual P3 gate, not a reproduced defect.

### Mentor flow

`publication/MENTOR_REVIEW_SEQUENCE_V16.md` is genuinely conversation-sized:
it starts with the human problem, delays the Map until the reader has a first
interpretation, tests breadth and proportion, then uses examples and claim
boundaries before closing with an invitation to challenge or expand. Its six
passes are a sequence of questions, not a sign-off ceremony. The response
sheet keeps the reader’s words separate from the author’s interpretation, and
the stop conditions prevent an Echo reduction, a peripheral-as-truth leap, a
plan-as-event claim, or a release request from silently becoming a result.

The minute ranges are explicitly editorial planning estimates. The sequence
does not claim observed reading time, comprehension, mentor agreement, or
completion because every route was opened.

**Disposition: Accepted.** The note adds a distinct private facilitation
surface without replacing the manuscript, cover note, or owner packet. Real
mentor judgment remains Deferred and unauthorized contact remains closed.

content. Removing it would not change the site, but would remove drift checks
for the unsent copy sizes, source links, null identity fields, and no-bitmap

social result. Likewise, the standalone HTML files are generated direct-open
representations promised by the repository package, not new public channels;
