## What these applications establish

They establish two concrete choices, a working representation fixture and a
specific Council correction from the broader audit. They do not establish that
the framework improves decisions or that either choice is commercially valuable.
Both initial recommendations survived inspection. The most defensible lesson
is to retain the small procedures, measure the next real use prospectively,
and let the research comparison determine whether their structure earns its
cost beyond a strong ordinary approach.
